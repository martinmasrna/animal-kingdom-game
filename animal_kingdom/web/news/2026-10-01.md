# 1 October 2026

## Rules

- An Apex Predator now eats a Stealth animal it lands on.
  Why: a predator eats what it lands on. Stealth only hides an animal from abilities that pick a target.
  Cards: Tiger, Eon, Polar Bear, Borealis, Black Panther

- Both players choose their opening hand at the same time. A player whose clock runs out three turns in a row loses.
  Why: a match against a person shouldn't wait on one player.

## New

- On a phone, press and hold a card to read it.

- A Magpie's discard or a Raven's shuffle lays your cards out in the middle, so it no longer looks like playing a card.

- New end-of-game screens: a win keeps the board lit, a loss lets it fade, and a ranked game counts your rating up or down.

- Opening the game in another tab or device takes you back into your match.

- Add friends from the leaderboard.

- Bots move as fast as you can follow.

- A phone's screen stays on while you play.

- When an animal's ability removes yours, that animal lunges at it.

- The Easy bot plays gentler, for anyone still learning the cards.

## Fixed

- A bot could take minutes over a move, or never make one.

- Signing in on a second device copied your starter decks again.

- Cards to choose from could run off the screen.

- The first click of a match was lost to the screen showing the two decks.
