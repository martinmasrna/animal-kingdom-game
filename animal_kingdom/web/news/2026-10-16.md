# 16 October 2026

## Rules

- Both players start with 4 cards, and the first player's first turn has one action instead of two.
  Why: going first was worth too much in a game that is usually decided by the sixth turn.
- Your hand holds up to 10 cards whatever puts a card there: a card made, returned, stolen or copied past 10 is removed, like a card drawn past 10.
## Keywords

- Hungry: at the start of your turn it eats that much of your food. If you can't feed it, it loses that much strength for good.
- Titan: playing it takes two actions. A free play can't pay for it.
- Flee: when an enemy covers it, it runs back to your hand.

## Interface

- Trigger marks on the board: an animal's moon (Dusk), sun (Dawn) or eye (when or whenever) lights up as its effect happens.
- An animal with Poison wears a skull on the board.
- The Remove Pile: open each side's removed cards from History, every card at its strength now.
- Collection: filter by habitat, and a card read large shows the cards it makes beside it.
