# 16 October 2026

## Rules

- Both players start with 4 cards, and the first player's first turn has one action instead of two.
  Why: going first was worth too much in a game that is usually decided by the sixth turn.
- An animal with Roam that is cut off from your den can still move back to your lines.
  Why: a cut-off roamer used to be stuck where it stood.
- Your hand holds up to 10 cards whatever puts a card there: a card made, returned, stolen or copied past 10 is removed, like a card drawn past 10.
  Why: cards that give you cards could fill a hand past its limit.

## Cards

- A new collection: twelve starter decks, with 107 new cards. Cats and Colony are rebuilt, and the other five come back renamed: Den Rush (Aggro), Roaming Pack (Canines), Birds & Snakes (Egg), Untouchables (Food) and Giants (Ramp); Circle of Life, Fish, Rodents, Primates and Hoofed are new.
  Why: seven decks were too few to play for long.
- Starter decks you never changed are replaced by the new ones. Decks you built or changed stay yours, minus any cards that left the game.
- Aurum lays a Golden Egg into your deck at Dawn instead of drawing a card. When you draw the egg, it is removed and you draw 2 cards.
  Why: a free card every turn was too strong in any deck that could find Aurum.

## New

- Trigger marks on the board: an animal's moon (Dusk), sun (Dawn) or eye (when or whenever) lights up as its effect happens.
- An animal with Poison wears a skull on the board.
- The Remove Pile: open each side's removed cards from History, every card at its strength now.
- Collection: filter by habitat, and a card read large shows the cards it makes beside it.
