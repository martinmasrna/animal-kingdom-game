# 8 October 2026

## Rules

- A player with no move passes their turn and the game goes on; it ends only when both players pass, one right after the other.
  Why: a player who had no move used to end the game on the spot, and could win it on food while their opponent was about to take their den.
- Your hand holds up to 10 cards, and you can draw even when it is full: each card drawn past 10 is removed.
  Why: a full hand used to forbid drawing, which could leave a player with no move while their deck still held cards.
