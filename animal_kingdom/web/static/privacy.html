Privacy

Animal Kingdom · last updated 29 September 2026

Animal Kingdom is a card game in development, run by one person, Martin Masrna. This page says what the game keeps about you and why. There are no ads, no trackers and no analytics services, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What the game keeps

  • Your profile: the name and #tag you play under, the decks you build, and a list of your finished matches.
  • Your games: the moves of every game you play against a person or a bot, and any notes you add during a game. These are used to design and balance the game.
  • If you sign in with Google or Discord: the id of that account and its email address (Google) or username (Discord), so the game knows it's you next time. The game never sees your password and asks for nothing else.
  • In your browser: a sign-in key that keeps you logged in on that device, and a few display preferences.

Where it lives

On the game's server, hosted by Fly.io in Frankfurt, Germany. Game records may also be copied to the developer's own computer for design work.

Deleting your data

Email martinmasrna.business@gmail.com with your name#tag and your profile, decks, sign-in links and match history will be deleted. Game records don't carry your name or account, so they stay.
