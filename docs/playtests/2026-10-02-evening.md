# Playtest read, 2026-10-02 evening

The games since the morning read: 109 by Laras (77) and Kalista (32), all ranked. Read the same way (`animal_kingdom/web/playtest.py`, then Luna in 14 batches of 8 games), every claim checked against the games. Martin's own 34 games are left out.

## Findings

### 1. Kalista holds her own on the ladder

She played only ranked tonight and went 16–16: 11–8 against the Normal bot, 5–6 against Expert, 0–2 against Martin. This morning she was 16–18 against Easy. The ladder pairs her with bots near her rating, and she wins half: the morning's worry (a newcomer losing most games) doesn't show here. Laras went 59–18.

### 2. Nothing new confused them

No repeated move that did nothing, no rule misread, no quit right after a surprise. Long thinks (25 s or more) came 8 times for Laras and 5 for Kalista, each followed by an ordinary move. Laras ended a turn with a move unused 3 times, once right before losing her den. Kalista's animals were removed the moment they landed 4 more times (the morning's finding 2, the cause not shown).

### 3. Kalista's Jaguar often finds nothing to remove

Jaguar ("remove an adjacent enemy of strength 4 or less") did nothing in 5 of her 11 plays; she plays it as a 5 that sometimes removes. No other card of hers fizzles that often (Lynx 2 of 29, Serval and Caracal 2 of 21). A fact, not a problem yet.

## The read itself

Two flaws in the playtest text made Luna report bugs that weren't, both fixed in `playtest.py`:

- What a turn's change sets off (start-of-turn food, a Bird Egg hatching) was listed under the move that ended the turn, so the opponent's income read as caused by your move. It now has its own line ("the turn passes").
- A Roar on the move that takes the den was flagged as doing nothing; the game is over, so it no longer is.

Luna's other "bugs" were card text it didn't know (Rhino, Brutus and Sirocco acting next to where they land, Eon shuffling itself back at the end of its turn). A result line's turn count is turns, the R number in the log is rounds (two turns each), so they never match. 384 of the older games no longer rebuild, since cards have changed since (Scout's three different cards among them).
