# Cats Midrange

Mono-Cat tempo and removal. Cats win the board by covering and removing: King Theron turns every Cat cover into a removal, Queen Adira turns every removal into a card, and Snow Leopard lets Cats cover at equal strength. Prince Leo and Princess Lea fetch each other from hand or deck, a designed twin pair rather than an open tutor.

Lion is the pool's only true vanilla body and the power reference for every ground unit (see [`../../design/principles.md`](../../design/principles.md)).
