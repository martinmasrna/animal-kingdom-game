## Who they are

Two partners, bonded for life (Martin, 2026-11-11). Equals: neither leads (Martin, 2026-11-11: "Equals").

## The names' shape

- The two names share no sound; the pair is bound by always being seen together (Martin, 2026-11-11: "that was my lean as well"). Arne and Arna were his earlier names, retracted because they sounded like siblings, like Leo and Lea.
- Northern (Martin, 2026-11-11: "northern fits them well").
- A name that does not exist as a real person's name yet sounds like one, bent out of a real name or a word, with the meaning underneath. Martin's models, 2026-11-11: Grimm ("literally one of the best names we have ... it works on both semantic/meaning level, and also on surface lexical level"; "the Grim" is a noun, "smrtka") and Geralt ("modification of real name 'Gerald', and while it's not a real name it SOUNDS like one").

What missed, in his words:

- Old Norse names nobody knows (Sigrun, Eskil, Yrsa): "slipping back into the 'meaningless gibberish' issue".
- Plain words as names (Frost, Saga, Storm): not names.
- Well-known northern names (Harald, Gustav, Frida): "just sound like human names plastered on top of the animal".
- Two finished things glued together (Kragnar, Preya): "too on the nose ... they sound more like attempts at puns rather than names".
