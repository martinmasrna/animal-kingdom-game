# [SPERM WHALE], the one who hunts the giants of the deep

> At night it goes down, and the biggest thing nearby doesn't come back up.

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 8. Titan. Dusk: remove the strongest adjacent enemy. Deck: Giants. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who it is

An old sperm whale bull, scarred across the head with round sucker marks the size of plates. A sperm whale dives a kilometre into the dark and hunts the giant squid there. This one hunts the biggest thing near it, every night. It doesn't eat the small. It goes down at dusk and takes whatever is largest, and in the morning it comes up with new scars and something missing from the sea around it.

It takes two of anyone's turns just to arrive. Nothing that size comes quickly.

## How it got this way

It has hunted the deep longer than the squid there have lived. Every scar on its head is one of them; it has more scars than any whale on record.

The card is this. **Titan** is its size: it costs the whole turn to bring in. **Dusk: remove the strongest adjacent enemy** is the night hunt: the biggest, the closest, every night.

## What the animals say

The deep animals tell stories about it, the way sailors told stories about the kraken. The squid are the ones who name it, and the name is a warning. The small fish on the surface don't fear it at all; it has never looked at them.

## One moment

Dawn on a flat sea. A huge grey head breaks the surface and blows. Across the forehead, fresh, are rings of torn skin in a line.

## Character

*Moby-Dick* turned around: the whale is the hunter, and the giant squid tells the stories.

## Stretch

None. The night hunt for the biggest thing near it is a sperm whale's life.
