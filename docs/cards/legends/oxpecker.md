# [OXPECKER], the bird on the giant's back

> When the rhino wins, she calls the others in.

Legendary, Savanna, strength 4. Bird. Flight. Whenever your animal of strength 8 or more covers an enemy, play an Oxpecker. Deck: Giants. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

An oxpecker, a small brown bird with a red beak who lives on the backs of the giants: rhinos, buffalo, giraffes. She eats their ticks and hisses when something creeps up. She rides the biggest of them, and she is the first to know when the big one has won something, because she was on its back when it did. Then she calls, and the whole flock comes in out of nowhere to the giant that won.

She is no fighter. She is the one who was there.

## How she got this way

Her flock rode a buffalo herd until the buffalo moved off and left them, and she was the one who found the rhino. A rhino doesn't want company and couldn't keep her off it. Her flock has eaten well ever since.

The card is this. **Whenever your animal of strength 8 or more covers an enemy** is a giant winning. **Play an Oxpecker** is her call and the flock arriving: a free body on the board, a bird that eats and draws. **Flight** is that she's never on the ground.

## What the animals say

The giants put up with her and call her by what she eats, rudely. The hyenas hate her, because she hisses and the rhino turns round. The other oxpeckers come when she calls.

## One moment

A rhino goes through a hyena in the dust. Before the dust settles there are a dozen red beaks on its back, and one of them is still calling.

## Character

Sancho Panza: rides with the big one, and is there first when the spoils come in.

## Names

- **Tick** (the giants): what she eats, said rudely and fondly at once. One beat, plain, a nickname with a seen thing.
- **Hiss** (the hyenas): the sound she makes when they come close, which brings the rhino round.

## Stretch

Small. Oxpeckers on rhinos, their alarm hiss and the flock are real. The card plays the common Oxpecker; reading that as the flock arriving is the only invention.
