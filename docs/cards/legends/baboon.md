## Where the name stands

Babaji, as a draft (Martin, 2026-11-12: "'Babaji' is good"): *baba*, the revered old man or holy man, with the respect suffix *-ji*. The Primates are one society (Martin: "all the primates, it makes sense to just put them all in the same society"), and they sound Indian: the guru whose students come out stronger, Hanuman, the *Bandar-log*. The agent's reading of him, which Martin took ("okay"): the old one the young come to, half wise and half mad, a little of Rafiki and Yoda. Kiron (Chiron one notch off) was on the card first: "I'm not too sold on that name ... We kind of just plastered it there". Bandar Baba and Gurudev were the other two put beside Babaji.
