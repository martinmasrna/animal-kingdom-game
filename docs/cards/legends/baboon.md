# Kiron, the wise old one

> The wise old dude, like the monkey from Lion King.

Legendary, Savanna. `Kiron`: Dawn: give 2 random animals in your hand +1 strength. Name: Kiron, draft (Martin, 2026-11-12). No legend is written yet.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

Martin, 2026-11-11: "if anything, he's the wise old dude, like the monkey from Lion King or something" (Rafiki). He is not a boss: the "old cunning boss watching over his land" line in the 2026-10-16 naming brief was an agent's, and Martin rejected it ("proper agent slop").

## Where the name stands

Kiron, as a draft. On 2026-11-12 the agent read him as the old one the young come to, half wise and half mad, a little of Rafiki and Yoda, his name so old nobody remembers who gave it (Martin: "okay"). Of Senex, Memor and Kiron (Chiron one notch off, the odd half-animal teacher of young heroes), Martin: "Kiron is the best of these, not 100% in love with it but Chiron is a good reference point as a character, yeah".
