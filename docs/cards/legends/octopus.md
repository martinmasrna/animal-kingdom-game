# [OCTOPUS], the one who knows where every legend sleeps

> Ask her, and she'll send word.

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 4. Roar: Scout a legendary animal. Decks: Untouchables, Hunters, Teeth and Claws, Menagerie. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

An old octopus in a den in the rocks. Around her den, as octopuses do, there is a garden of things she has collected: a shark's tooth, a turtle's shell plate, the earbone of a whale, a broken claw. Each one is something she knows about someone big. She knows where every one of the sea's legends sleeps, and the ones on land too, as far as anything in the sea can know them. Ask her for one, and she'll send word, and three come to look at you. One stays.

She has no side. She has dealings with everyone. That is why she's in so many decks.

## How she got this way

She lives long for an octopus, which is still not long, and she has spent all of it learning. An octopus is the cleverest animal in the sea, and she is the cleverest octopus. She kept everything she found, and she found out that the things big animals lose tell you where they are.

The card is this. **Scout a legendary animal** is her sending word: three legends hear of you, and one comes. She doesn't fight; strength 4 is an octopus that doesn't need to.

## What the animals say

The fish say her name and nod at the rocks: she's in there, ask her. The legends themselves know her name too, and that is what makes her one.

## One moment

A grouper hangs outside a crack in the rocks, surrounded by teeth and bones laid out like a shop window. An arm comes out of the dark, touches the grouper's head once, and goes back in.

## Character

Winston, who runs the Continental in *John Wick*: he knows where every legend sleeps and can get word to any of them. A fit with Ash, who is John Wick.

## Names

- **Inky** (the fish, fondly): the real octopus who escaped from New Zealand's National Aquarium in 2016, across the floor and down a drainpipe to the sea. A real animal's name, the way Scarface is the real Mara lion. Its risk is that it reads cute.
- **Tangle** (the fish): eight arms in the dark of a crack. Plain, a seen thing, and harder.

## Stretch

Medium. Octopus intelligence and den gardens are real. The fixer who calls legends is the legend's invention, to carry a tutor.
