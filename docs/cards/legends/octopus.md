# Madame Octavia, the fixer

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 4. Roar: Scout a legendary animal. Name: Madame Octavia, draft (Martin, 2026-11-12).

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

The fixer who knows where every legend of the sea sleeps and sends word; she takes no side and has dealings with everyone. The film equivalent from the 2026-11-05 legend (agent-written, thrown away with the others on 2026-11-09) was Winston, who runs the Continental in *John Wick*. Put to Martin on 2026-11-12 beside the alien loner whose name nobody can say (the reading behind Mnuul, Uulm and Hlua on the shelf): "I think psychology-wise the fixer fits better (not that 'Winston' as a name invokes that lol)".

## The name

French, a Madame: the concierge, the salon hostess, Madame who runs the house; smooth on the surface, sharp underneath. Polynesian was tried first for the whole sea and dropped for her (Martin: "it fits only surface-wise but not psychology-wise"); she is a loner, so she need not share the fish's culture. On "Madame": "haha, 'Madame' sounds good, yeah". Of Octavie, Poulpe, Nacre and Sépia, Martin: "Madame Octavia I think works better, no?" Octavia, "the eighth", carries the eight arms.
