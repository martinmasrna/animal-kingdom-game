# [BOAR], the one who turns the meadow over

> Where he fed last night, nothing feeds today.

Legendary, Forest, strength 5. Hoofed. Your opponent's regions produce 5 less food. Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

An old boar, heavy in the shoulder, with tusks worn short from digging. A boar roots: it pushes its snout under the turf and turns the ground over for grubs and bulbs. Most boars do this in the forest. He does it in the meadows where everyone else grazes. A night of his feeding leaves a pasture looking ploughed, the grass upside down, the roots in the air. The deer and the herds come out at dawn and find nothing to eat.

He isn't fighting anyone. He's hungry, and the grass was in the way of what's under it.

## How he got this way

A wet autumn drove the grubs up close to the surface in the open meadows, and he found that the digging there was easier than in the forest. He never went back. Every year the meadows he visits get worse for everyone else and better for him.

The card is this. **Your opponent's regions produce 5 less food** is what the meadow looks like the morning after him. He doesn't take the food; he spoils it for the ones who were going to eat there.

## What the animals say

The grazers say his name as a curse. They have no word for what he does except his name. His own sounder say it with pride: the old one always finds food. The two never mean the same animal.

## One moment

Morning in a forest clearing. The grass is turned over in long dark strips, still steaming. A doe stands at the edge, looking at it, and doesn't step in.

## Character

The Calydonian Boar of Greek myth, without the goddess: sent to ruin the fields of Calydon, and remembered for that, not for anything it killed. Famous boars to stay clear of: Okkoto (*Princess Mononoke*), Pumbaa.

## Names

- **Blight** (the grazers): the word for what kills a field, from the animals' own world (blight is on leaves, not in a toolshed). One beat, hard, said as a curse.
- **Grubber** (his own sounder): the one who always finds the grubs, a fond name. Plainer, and closer to a comic register.

## Stretch

Small. Rooting really does ruin pasture, and wild boar damage to meadows is a real problem in Europe. Note that the card is Hoofed and the herds page lists him as "Unplaced" (`docs/design/societies/herds.md`, not settled). This legend makes him the herds' problem, not their member.
