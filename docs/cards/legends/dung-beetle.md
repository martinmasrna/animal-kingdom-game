# El'Rahhal, the beetle his kind walk behind

> He rolled farther than any of us, and came back.

Legendary, Savanna, strength 4. Evolves at 10 food eaten by your Hungry animals; three later forms add, in turn, "Roar: draw a card", "remove an adjacent enemy" and "play another animal". Name: El'Rahhal (draft, Martin, 2026-11-09: "El'Rahhal is good"), Arabic for "the great wanderer", on all four forms. Stage words (Martin, 2026-11-09): El'Rahhal: Birth, Journey, Trial, Ascension.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

A dung beetle who really lived, and whom the dung beetles still tell as their great legend, the way the British tell King Arthur (Martin, 2026-11-09). The name is the one his own kind gave him and still say, in their own old tongue, which sounds Egyptian, North African, Arabic: the scarab of ancient Egypt is the feeling, not the story.

## His life, one form a chapter

1. **Birth.** He hatched inside a ball of dung the herds left behind, the smallest of his brood. He grows only when the giants feed: the savanna's plenty is what he is made of. (The base card, evolving on food your Hungry animals eat.)
2. **Journey.** He leaves and rolls his first ball, at night, steering by the stars in a dead straight line, as real dung beetles steer by the Milky Way, and goes farther than any beetle had. The draw is what he finds out there.
3. **Trial.** Thieves come for his ball: beetles that never roll their own and fight to take another's, as real ones do. He fights them off and keeps it. (Removes an adjacent enemy.)
4. **Ascension.** He comes back, and the others start to follow him. He is no longer one beetle with a ball but the one his kind walk behind, the one the young are told about. (Plays another animal: the ones who come with him.)

## Dissent log

- The ending where his ball rises and becomes the sun was dropped (Martin, 2026-11-09: "trying way too hard to fit the 'becomes the sun' thing"). His ascension is becoming the legend his kind follow, not becoming the sun.
