# [DUNG BEETLE], the one who follows the giants

> The more the elephants eat, the bigger he gets.

Legendary, Savanna, strength 4. Beetle. Evolves at 10 food eaten by your Hungry animals; three more forms, at 25 and 40, each drawing, and the later ones removing an adjacent enemy and playing another animal. Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

A dung beetle who lives behind the giants. Where the elephants and rhinos go, they leave mountains of dung, and every dung beetle on the plain comes to it. He comes first and leaves last. He rolls the biggest ball, so big he has to climb on it to see where he's going. At night, he steers it by the stars.

He does nothing for the giants and they don't know he exists. But the more they eat, the more there is for him, and he has grown as they have.

## How he got this way

He was born inside a ball of elephant dung, as all of them are. His herd of giants never left, so his ball never ran out. Each season he was bigger, and the other beetles started giving way.

The card is this. **Evolves at food eaten by your Hungry animals** is him growing on what the giants leave: the hungrier they are, the faster he grows. **Draw a card** is what he finds in the dung. The later forms' **remove an adjacent enemy** and **play another animal** are the stretch below.

## What the animals say

The giants don't know him. The other beetles do: he is the one whose ball you don't try to steal. The plain's small animals say that wherever he is rolling, the giants have passed by, and they keep away from both.

## One moment

Night on the plain, the elephant herd asleep. A ball of dung the size of a fist is rolling east in a dead straight line, and the beetle pushing it stops, climbs on top, looks up at the Milky Way, and goes on.

## Character

Mother Courage, in Brecht's play: she follows the war with her wagon and lives off it, and she gets on fine as long as the war goes on.

## Names

- **Scarab** (the beetles): the old word for the sacred dung beetle, the one people painted on tombs. It sounds like a name, and it is the beetle's own word in English. Its risk is that it names the kind, not the individual.
- **Roller** (the plain's small animals): what they see him do. Plain and seen. It may read as on the nose, the way Thickskin did.

## Stretch

Large, for the later forms. Growing on the giants' food is real and fits the first form well. Removing an enemy and an extra placement have no beetle behind them, except a ball so big it rolls over things. Martin, 2026-10-27, on an earlier draw: "not sure if the legendary dung beetle is even legendary-worthy, it's just a boring draw engine just for this deck specifically". The legend doesn't answer that, and a legend can't fix a card.

## Open

- Navigating by the Milky Way is real (South African dung beetles, 2013 study), and is the one thing here that could carry a painting.
