# [TUNA], the one who comes back with the run

> When she comes in, every coast that's yours eats.

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 5. Fish. Roar: for each region you control, draw a card and gain 5 food. Deck: Fish. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A bluefin tuna, huge, warm-blooded, built like a bullet, who crosses an ocean every year and comes back. When the bluefin come through the strait in early summer, they bring the whole sea with them: the shoals they chase, the birds over the shoals, the dolphins under the birds. She leads the run. Wherever the coast is held when she arrives, everything on it eats.

She doesn't stay. She was never going to.

## How she got this way

She has made the crossing more times than any bluefin in the sea, out to the far feeding grounds and back to where she was born. The coasts learned the season she comes, and wait for it.

The card is this. **For each region you control, draw a card and gain 5 food** is the run arriving: every stretch of coast that's yours gets fed and gets news. The more you hold, the bigger the feast. She is a Fish who doesn't need other Fish (Martin, 2026-10-29: the Fish legendaries are "universal token/region control legendaries not forcing you to play fishes").

## What the animals say

The coast's birds and seals say her name the way farmers say the name of the rain. It means the season has turned.

## One moment

A summer morning at the strait. The water goes dark with fish, the sky goes white with gannets, and somewhere under all of it, very fast, is something the size of a seal.

## Character

Odysseus coming home, without the suitors: a long voyage, and something for every harbour still loyal to him.

## Names

- **Runner** (the coast): the run is here. Plain, said with relief.
- **Longway** (the coast): where she has come from. A compound, softer, and less likely to read as a job.

## Stretch

Medium. The migration and the feast that follows it are real. The draw is the legend's reading of "news from the run". A tuna list got a "nope" from Martin in the naming record (2026-10-14).

## Open

- Nothing beyond the names.
