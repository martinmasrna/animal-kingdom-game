# [BLACK WOLF], the pack that never stops moving

> Every wolf that runs with him comes home bigger.

Legendary, Forest, strength 6. Canine. Roam. Whenever a Canine roams, give it +1 strength and draw a card. Decks: Roaming Pack. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

A big black wolf who leads a pack that never stands still. Most packs hold a territory; his runs. They cross three valleys in a night and are somewhere else by morning, and every wolf that runs with him comes back stronger and knowing more than it did. The pack doesn't belong to a place. It belongs to the running.

He isn't the strongest wolf in the forest. He is the one who never stops.

## How he got this way

He was born into a pack that lost its territory to a bigger one, and instead of fighting for it back he took them away. They found that a pack on the move eats better than a pack at home, because the deer don't know they're coming. He has been on the move ever since.

The card is this. **Roam** is him moving. **Whenever a Canine roams, give it +1 strength and draw a card** is what running with him gives every wolf of his: more strength and one more thing it knows. It counts him too.

## What the animals say

The deer in every valley say his name the night he arrives, because last night he was three valleys away. The other packs say it with dislike: he never holds anything and never stays to fight for it, and he takes what's theirs anyway.

## One moment

Moonlight on snow. A line of tracks runs across a frozen lake and up into the trees on the other side. In front of all of them is a set larger than the others.

## Character

The Mongol horde: never stands still, and every ride makes it bigger.

## Names

- **Shadow Paw** (the deer): on Martin's shelf, from his voice note of 2026-10-16, beside Moon Paw. A black wolf at night is a shadow, and the paw is the track he leaves behind. Its pair is on the [WHITE WOLF].
- **Smoke** (the other packs): black, moving, and gone when you reach for it. One beat.

## Stretch

Small. Wolves do travel long distances in a night, and black wolves are real (Yellowstone has many). Getting stronger by roaming is the legend's reading.

## Open

- Whether the black and the white wolf are a pair, kin or rivals is Martin's call; the shelf pair of names fits any of the three.
- Famous wolves to stay clear of: Akela, Sirius (both ruled out by Martin), Ghost (*Game of Thrones*).
