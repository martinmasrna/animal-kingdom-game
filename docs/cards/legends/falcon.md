# [FALCON], the one who flies the siege

> She is at the den before the defenders see the raid.

Legendary, Forest, strength 5. Bird. Flight. Roar: draw a card for each ally next to your opponent's den. Deck: Den Rush. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A peregrine falcon, the fastest animal there is. When the raiders are at the gate of someone's den, she is the one who goes over the top: down onto the den mouth in a dive nobody can follow, takes one look, and is back with the raiders before the defenders can call out. The more of the raiders are already at the gate, the more she can tell them. Alone, she sees nothing worth telling.

## How she got this way

She hunted the cliffs above the forest dens for years, and the raiders learned to watch where she dived. Where the falcon went down, there was something to find. They started going where she pointed, and she started pointing.

The card is this. **Flight** is the dive, past every wall. **Draw a card for each ally next to your opponent's den** is what she brings back from the den mouth: a word for every raider at the gate.

## What the animals say

The raiders call her by what they see her do. The defenders call her by what they see, which is almost nothing: a shadow that's gone before you look up. Both use the name to mean "they're here."

## One moment

The den's sparrows are calling on the wall. One of them stops. A grey shape has gone through the place where it was, and is already rising on the other side.

## Character

Cher Ami, the pigeon who flew a message through the guns at the Argonne, turned around: this one flies over the siege and comes back with what the attackers need.

## Names

- **Blink** (the defenders): you blink and she's gone. One beat, plain, a word from the animals' world. It may read as cute for a 5-strength killer.
- **Stoop** (the raiders): the falcon's dive. It is the real word for it, but it comes from falconry, so it's on the edge of "a word the animals can't know". Mark it.

## Stretch

Medium. A peregrine diving onto a den is real; the scout who brings back news is the legend's invention, to carry a draw.
