# [WILDEBEEST], the old cow who knows where the grass is

> Wherever she stands, the grass is better.

Legendary, Savanna, strength 5. Hoofed. Your regions produce 5 more food. Decks: Hoofed, Small Fry. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

An old wildebeest cow, grey in the face, who has walked the migration more times than any animal in the herd. Every herd has old ones who remember the route; she remembers it best. When the rains fail and the herd stops at the escarpment not knowing where to go, she turns where nobody has gone in nine years, and two days later they are in green grass up to the knee.

She doesn't make the grass. She knows where it is. The herd that stands with her eats better than any other on the plain.

## How she got this way

She was a calf in the year the rains failed for the whole plain, and the herd she was in survived because an old cow took them north. She was right behind that cow the whole way. She never forgot the turns.

The card is this. **Your regions produce 5 more food** is the ground she brought you to: wherever you hold grass, it's the better grass, because she chose it.

## What the animals say

The herd says her name with trust, the way people say the name of a guide on a mountain. The herds page proposes this job for her, "the memory of the route" (`docs/design/societies/herds.md`, a proposal, not settled). The lions know only that the herd with her in it is always where the food is, and so are they.

## One moment

The herd at the edge of a brown plain, all standing, heads down at dust. One grey cow lifts her head, sniffs the wind, and walks left. A thousand animals follow her.

## Character

Moses in the wilderness, without the commandments: the one who knows the way to the good land.

## Names

- **Rain** (the herd): what she follows and what she finds. One beat, plain, and it is what the herd wants most.
- From the record: **Haldaran**, which Martin gave "9/10" for the wildebeest in the herds' naming sitting (2026-10-21), and which was never applied. It is not English; it is here because it is the best reaction in the record for this card.

## Stretch

None. The old animals who remember the route are real, and the food bonus is the route.
