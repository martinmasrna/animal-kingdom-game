# Xanthe, the one the meadow is better for

> Everything else is just better for it having been there.

Legendary, Meadow, five cards and one life, strength 0 at every stage: Xanthe: Egg, Caterpillar, Chrysalis, Butterfly, Monarch. It evolves each dawn and whenever it gains strength; each stage draws and strengthens the animals still in hand; Stealth from the chrysalis, Flight from the butterfly. Name: Xanthe (draft, Martin, 2026-11-10: "let's do Xanthe"), a Greek given name meaning "golden".

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

One pearl of an egg on a milkweed leaf in low gold light. Every dawn it is something else, and each form holds more of the light, until the wings are stained glass. It is never strong itself. Nothing in the meadow needs to get out of its way; everything else is just better for it having been there (Martin, 2026-11-10: "a good way to describe it").

The namers are the small things of the meadow that watched the same leaf every morning, from egg to monarch.

## The name's shape

In "Name: Stage" the stage is a type word, so the name has to read as one individual's name and nothing else (Martin, 2026-11-10, on Tiny and Wisp: "they don't sound like names, but like animal/creature types ... the X part stands out more than the name itself").
