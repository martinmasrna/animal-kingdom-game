# [BUTTERFLY], the one the meadow walked over

> Every morning she is something else.

Legendary, Meadow, strength 0. Dawn: evolve. Whenever this gains strength, evolve. Becomes Caterpillar, Chrysalis, Butterfly and Monarch, each drawing and giving strength to your hand. Deck: Primates. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A caterpillar on a milkweed stalk, small, striped, eating. The meadow walks past her. The ants go over her. Then one morning there is a green case where she was, then wings, and at the end a monarch: the orange-and-black butterfly that crosses a continent. Everyone who walked over the caterpillar remembers it.

She is never the same thing two mornings running, and everything around her changes with her: whoever stands near her when she turns is a little more than before.

## How she got this way

She didn't do anything. It is what she is. But most caterpillars on a stalk are eaten before they finish, and she wasn't, because milkweed makes her poison to birds. She ate the one plant that protects her, and kept eating, and woke up as the next thing.

The card is this. **Dawn: evolve** is the morning she is something new. **Whenever this gains strength, evolve** is that anything that feeds her moves her on. Each stage's Roar draws and puts strength into the hand: the meadow around her growing as she does. The **Monarch** is the last form and the name of the real species, and it is a title as well.

## What the animals say

The meadow's small animals say they knew her when she was a caterpillar. All of them say it. Very few of them did.

## One moment

The ants that marched over a striped grub for a week stop at the foot of the stalk, because the case at the top is splitting, and something orange is coming out.

## Character

Aladdin: the street kid nobody looked at twice, who ends as royalty, and the whole market says it knew him. Famous caterpillars to stay clear of: the Very Hungry Caterpillar, the hookah-smoking one in *Alice*.

## Names

- **Milkweed** (the meadow): the plant she lives on and the thing that keeps her alive. A plant name, as in Martin's braindump ("a bunch of them can be named after flowers and plants"). It belongs to the caterpillar and stays with the monarch.
- **Stripes** (the ants): what they saw when she was nothing, said with a shrug. Plainer and smaller; it works if the joke is that the monarch is still called by her grub name.

## Stretch

Medium. The four stages are real and the evolution fits perfectly. The draw and the strength to the hand have no butterfly behind them; "the meadow grows with her" is the legend's invention to carry them.

## Open

- The four forms already carry their own card names (Caterpillar, Chrysalis, Butterfly, Monarch). Whether the legendary's name sits on the first form only or on all of them is a card question, not this file's.
