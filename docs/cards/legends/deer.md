# [DEER], the hind who leads the running

> Every one she brings home runs better the next time.

Legendary, Forest, strength 3. Hoofed. Flee. Roar: draw 2 animals with Flee. Whenever one of your animals flees, give it +2 strength. Deck: Hoofed. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

An old red deer hind. In a real deer herd the leader is not a stag but an old female who knows the ground, and she is that. When the wolves come, she runs first and the others follow her, through the bramble where a wolf can't keep pace, across the stream where it loses the scent, into the beechwood where she knows every way out. She has never stood and fought. She has never lost anyone who stayed behind her.

And everyone who runs behind her comes back harder to catch.

## How she got this way

She was a yearling in a bad winter when the wolves took her mother. She ran without knowing where, and found she knew. Every chase since has taught her another way out, and she teaches it to whoever is running beside her.

The card is this. **Flee** is her running, back to safety rather than dying. **Draw 2 animals with Flee** is her calling the runners to her. **Whenever one of your animals flees, give it +2 strength** is what a chase under her leaves in the runner: it comes back knowing the way, and the next wolf has to work harder.

## What the animals say

The hinds call her by the name of the way out she likes best. The wolves call her something else, said tired: the one who takes them away. Neither side has seen her caught.

## One moment

The wolves come out of the beechwood with their tongues out and nothing in their mouths. On the far hill, a line of hinds stands looking back, the old one in front.

## Character

Big X in *The Great Escape*: he organizes every breakout, and every man who got out once runs better the next time.

## Names

- **Bramble** (the hinds): the way out where wolves can't follow, a plant name from the forest floor. It also has the scratch in it, which is a cost of her escapes.
- **Old Hind** (the wolves): plain and tired, the shelf's "Old [XYZ]" shape. Its weakness is that "hind" is a word a non-native player may not know.

## Stretch

Small. Matriarch-led deer herds and fleeing as survival are real. Fleers growing stronger is the legend's reading: a real deer doesn't get stronger from being chased, but a herd that survives learns its escape routes.
