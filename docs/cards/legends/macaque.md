# [MACAQUE], the boss of the temple steps

> Whatever you have, he'll have as much.

Legendary, City, strength 2. Primate. Roar: draw cards until you have as many as your opponent. Deck: Rodents. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

A macaque who runs a crew on the old temple steps at the edge of the City. Martin's own reading (2026-10-16): a crime boss, not a glutton. He is small and he doesn't need to be big. He takes. Whatever another animal is carrying, he wants as much as they have, and he gets it, because the crew comes in behind him. He never leaves a table with less than anyone at it. That is the whole rule, and nobody on the steps has ever seen it broken.

He doesn't eat most of what he takes. He keeps it, trades it, gives it to the crew. Taking is the point.

## How he got this way

He grew up on the steps where the macaques rob whoever comes up them, and learned early that the trick is not to take things but to take them back: snatch something, then sell it back for more than it was worth. He was better at it than the others, and then they worked for him.

The card is this. **Draw cards until you have as many as your opponent** is his rule: he evens the score, and he does it from behind. The further behind he is, the more he takes. Strength 2 is a small monkey who doesn't fight his own fights.

## What the animals say

The City's small animals say his name with a quick look over the shoulder, the way you say a boss's name. The rats' crews respect his steps and he respects their granary; it's a different crew on a different street (`docs/cards/legends/rat.md`). The pigeons say he has never lost anything he wanted.

## One moment

A pigeon on the temple steps with a crust. A small brown hand comes from nowhere and the crust is gone. The pigeon looks round, and the macaque is sitting three steps up, not eating it, watching the pigeon, waiting to see what else it has.

## Character

Fanucci, the Black Hand in *The Godfather Part II*: he wets his beak in everyone's business, and his cut is always even with the biggest share.

## Names

- **Fingers** (the street): a classic mob nickname from a seen thing, the quick hand. Plain English, and it says who he is with no explanation.
- **Cheeks** (the street): macaques carry food in their cheek pouches, a seen mark. Fatcheeks was rejected in the record on 2026-10-14 ("garbage... lazy"), so this is close to a known miss. Marked.

## Stretch

Small. Real temple macaques do rob and trade back (Uluwatu, Bali), and the parity draw is a boss who takes until he is even.
