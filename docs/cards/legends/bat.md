# [BAT], the ear of the City at night

> Ask him in the morning what happened.

Legendary, City, strength 1. Flight. Dawn: draw a card. Deck: Birds & Snakes. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

A small bat who lives under the old bridge in the City. All night he hunts moths through the streets, and a bat hears everything: every rat on the move, every cat on a wall, every dog let out. He comes home at first light, hangs upside down under the bridge, and the pigeons come and ask him what happened in the night. He always knows. He is too small for anyone to bother eating, and he never takes sides. He just tells whoever asks.

## How he got this way

The bridge was the only roost left when the old church was closed up. A hundred bats used to hang there; he is one of the last. With the others gone, he had the whole night to himself and no one to tell, and the City found out that the bat under the bridge knew things.

The card is this. **Flight** is the night streets. **Dawn: draw a card** is what he brings home every morning: one more thing you didn't know. Strength 1 is a bat, and no one wants him dead.

## What the animals say

The pigeons call him the night's ear and come to him at dawn. The rats like him less, because he has seen where they go. The cats can't reach him and stopped trying. Every crew in the City has asked him something at least once.

## One moment

First light under the bridge. A small dark shape folds its wings and hangs. A pigeon lands on the rail above it and waits, head cocked.

## Character

Varys and his little birds in *A Song of Ice and Fire*, as one bat: he knows, and he tells. Famous bats to stay clear of: Stellaluna, Batman.

## Names

- **Echo** (the pigeons): what a bat hunts by, and what he does with the night, saying it back in the morning. Echo was one of the rejected names for the mimic octopus in the naming record (2026-10-14). Here it has a reason it had nothing like there.
- **Whisper** (the rats): how what he knows travels, said with less love.

## Stretch

Small. A bat at night and a draw at dawn is a clean fit. The informant is the legend's own addition; a real bat just eats moths.

## Open

- Whether the City exists as a society is open; the rat's legend says "the City" too, and this file uses it the same way.
