# [RODENT], the hoarder who makes today count twice

> Whatever came in today, she puts away twice.

Legendary, Forest, strength 3. Rodent. Roar: gain as much food as you gained this turn. Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Which animal

Martin left the species open (2026-10-29: "just put it as [RODENT]"). The legend proposes the **common hamster**, the wild European one, because no rodent hoards like it: a single hamster has been dug out of its burrow with tens of kilos of grain stored away, and it carries the harvest in its cheek pouches, load after load. The catch is habitat: the wild hamster lives in fields and steppe, not forest. The card says Forest. If the forest matters more than the hoard, a red squirrel or a dormouse fits the place, but the hoard less (and Yuki is a squirrel already).

## Who she is

A hamster, fat-cheeked and fierce, who lives at the forest edge where the fields begin. All day she carries in whatever the day gives, and every time a good day comes in, she makes it twice: two loads for every one, back down the burrow, until her stores are the deepest in the wood. She doesn't share. She will fight anything that comes down her hole, and a wild hamster will fight a dog.

## How she got this way

She was born in a famine year, the last of a litter, and she was the one who lived, because she was the one who ate everything. She never stopped acting as if it was a famine year.

The card is this. **Gain as much food as you gained this turn** is the second load: whatever the day brought in, she brings in again. On a poor day it is nothing; on a good day it is a lot. She doubles, she never makes.

## What the animals say

The forest's small animals say her name the way you say the name of a rich neighbour who never lends anything. The foxes know her hole and have learned not to dig there.

## One moment

Autumn dusk at the field's edge. A small round shape runs into a hole with her cheeks so full her head is twice its size. She comes back out thin, and runs off for more.

## Character

Joseph in Egypt: he stores the good years twice over, so the famine finds the granary full.

## Names

- **Stash** (the forest): what she has, and what she does. One beat, plain.
- **Twice** (the forest, with a grin): what she makes of a good day. An unusual word as a name, and it says the card.

## Stretch

Small for the effect (the hoard and the cheeks are real), and the habitat is the honest problem above.

## Open

- The species, above all.
