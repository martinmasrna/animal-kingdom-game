# The cuckoo, the spoiled boy who never grew up

> Just doing whatever they want and not caring who they hurt in the process.

Legendary, Meadow. `[CUCKOO]`: Roar: remove an adjacent animal of strength 4 or less; if it was yours, play another animal. Name: open. No legend is written yet.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

Martin, 2026-11-11: "some ultimately selfish character, without morals, just doing whatever they want and not caring who they hurt in the process. perhaps a manchild, grown up spoiled kid, something like that". Of the characters put to him, Joffrey (Game of Thrones) and Dudley Dursley fit; Commodus fits less well ("maybe because those two are young?").

## Who names him

The agent's reading, put to Martin on 2026-11-12: the warblers who raised him. He hatched in their nest, pushed their own chicks out and grew many times their size, and they kept feeding him; his name is the pet name the tiny doting mother gave her darling boy, the way Petunia calls Dudley "Duddykins", and he never outgrew it. The sweet name on a brute is the character. Martin's reaction: "okay, let's try".

## Where the name stands

Open. Bent versions of dusty English boys' names (Obrey, Humfrey, Godrey, Dudly, Budley, Cubbert, Eustas, Pursey, Wilfry) did not sound like him (Martin, 2026-11-11: "no"). Words about the bird itself (egg, nest, gowk) were dropped as material: they lead to puns.
