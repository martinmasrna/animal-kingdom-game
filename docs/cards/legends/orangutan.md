# Nani, the grandmother

Legendary, Jungle, Primate. Roar: give all animals in your hand +1 strength. Name: Nani, draft (Martin, 2026-11-12).

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

The card was the Gorilla legendary until 2026-11-12, when Martin found the effect a raiser's, not a fighter's ("for baboon it fits, but for gorilla I'm not really sure"). The agent's reading, which he took: the orangutan mother, who carries and teaches her child for seven or eight years, longer than any animal but us; every animal in your hand comes out of her care stronger. The Gorilla waits for an effect that is him. She needs her own painting; the gorilla's stands in.

## The name

The Primates are one society and sound Indian, beside Babaji. Vanari, Vanamma and Ammaji were "gibberish"; Naniji and Maaji ended in Babaji's *-ji* ("ideally it wouldn't end in '-ji' just so we're not confusing the two too much"). Martin brought Nana, then Nani: the Hindi word every child calls their mother's mother, a form of address that works as a name in the family, the way Granny or Gramma Tala does.
