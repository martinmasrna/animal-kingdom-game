# [MOLE], the one who digs the way through

> Nobody saw him. Somebody came out of the ground anyway.

Legendary, Meadow, strength 1. Roar: draw a card, play another animal, then discard a card. Decks: Den Rush, Small Fry, Thick Skin. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

A mole, nearly blind, with pink spade hands and soft black fur, who has never been seen above ground by anyone who could tell about it. Under the meadow he has dug more tunnel than any mole alive. He digs the way through for others: a tunnel opens, and someone small comes out of it where nobody expected anyone. Each time, something is left in the dirt behind: a mole tunnel takes and gives.

He does it for whoever is on his side. He doesn't ask what they want it for.

## How he got this way

The hawks took every mole that came up for air in his first spring. He learned not to come up at all. Everything he does, he does from below, and the meadow's small animals found out that the mole would take them under the hawks if they asked.

The card is this. **Play another animal** is someone else coming out of his tunnel, in the same action. **Draw a card, then discard a card** is the digging: he turns up something new and leaves something behind in the dirt. Strength 1 is a mole.

## What the animals say

The hawks have a word for him, which is no word: when they ask who did it, the answer is nobody. The small animals of the meadow say his name only underground.

## One moment

A hawk hangs over the meadow, watching an empty field. In the middle of it, a small heap of earth rises, crumbles, and a hare climbs out and is gone.

## Character

Danny the Tunnel King in *The Great Escape*: he digs, and gets other people through. Famous mole to stay clear of: Mole in *The Wind in the Willows*.

## Names

- **Nobody** (the hawks): Odysseus's trick name for the Cyclops, which every reader of the story knows, and it fits a mole no one has seen. It cites; Martin may hear the Odyssey.
- **Pinkhands** (the meadow's small animals): the one seen mark. A compound, the shape that went either way in the record (Greywhisker gold, Snowtail out).

## Stretch

Medium. The tunnel that lets someone else through fits the extra placement. The draw and the discard are the legend's reading (what the digging turns up and what it buries), not anything a mole does.

## Open

- Velvet, the obvious mark for mole fur, is a human cloth and is left out under Martin's absolute.
