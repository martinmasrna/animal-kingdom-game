# [BEE], the nurse who makes the jelly

> Every one she feeds grows into more than it was.

Legendary, Meadow, strength 1. Colony. Roar: if your starting deck has no animals of strength 4 or more, give your animals +2 strength (wherever they are). Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A nurse bee. In a real hive the young workers feed the larvae, and the feed is royal jelly; a larva fed only jelly grows into a queen instead of a worker. She is the one who made the queen. She is old for a bee and still feeds, and what she makes is stronger than any other nurse's. Wherever she goes in the nest, the small ones grow.

She feeds small things. That is all she knows how to do. A nest with something big in it doesn't need her, and she doesn't know what to do with it.

## How she got this way

The hive lost its queen in a hard spring. A hive without a queen dies in weeks. The nurses picked a few young larvae and fed them jelly and nothing else, and one of them lived and became the queen. The nurse who fed her never went back to being an ordinary worker. The court calls her by name, which a worker never gets.

The card is this. **+2 strength to your animals, wherever they are** is her jelly reaching every cell of the nest, in hand and deck as well as on the board. **No animals of strength 4 or more** is a nest of small things, the only kind she knows how to feed.

## What the animals say

The workers don't say her name; they don't say anyone's. The court does, because she made one of them. The meadow's other insects know only that a nest with her in it grows fast.

## One moment

Inside the comb, dark and warm. One bee goes from cell to cell, head down in each, and the white grubs in them are bigger than the ones in the next row.

## Character

Molly Weasley: she feeds every child in the house as her own, and they all grow on it.

## Names

- **Ambrosia** (the court): the food of the gods, which is what royal jelly is to a bee. An English word with a classical sound, which matches the court names already on cards (Melissia, Solenia). Its risk is that it reads as a word, not a name.
- **Jelly** (the meadow, with a grin): plain and fond. It works only if Martin wants a softer, funnier card; the Bumbleton register shows the hive can carry one joke.

## Stretch

Medium. Royal jelly making a queen is real, and feeding as a buff fits. The small-deck condition has no bee behind it: the legend reads it as "a nest of small things", which is an invention to explain a deckbuilding rule. The hive page's proposal (`docs/design/societies/hive.md`, not settled) says a worker has no name; this legend gets around that by putting her in the court, and that is a choice Martin can undo.

## Open
