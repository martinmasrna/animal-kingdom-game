# Santa Sardinia, the fish's own saint

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 1. Fish. Santa Sardinia and your adjacent Fish count as 2 Fish. Name: Santa Sardinia, draft (Martin, 2026-11-12).

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

Not one sardine but a figure from the fish's own mythology. Martin, 2026-11-12: "for the fishes, like their strength is in numbers ... I'm not sure whether it makes too much sense to even to like make this a specific individual. It's more like a concept or feeling, which makes me want to go in the route of making her like a figure from mythology of the animals themselves", the way El'Rahhal is a dung beetle from the beetles' own stories; as an individual, "why would each fish count as two? It just reads a bit weird." The agent's reading from there: the shoals' saint, around whom two fish become four (Martin: "hm, okay..."). The name is the fish's, never the fishermen's ("These names are not supposed to be given by humans").

The fish sound Iberian (Martin, 2026-11-12: "sounds about right", for the sea's map: the fish Iberian, Madame Octavia French, Borvald Norse).

## The name

Martin's own: "how about 'Santa Sardinia'? too on the nose?" Sardinia is the island the sardine is named after.
