# [SARDINE], the one in the middle of the shoal

> Next to her, every fish is two.

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 1. Fish. [SARDINE] and your adjacent Fish count as 2 Fish. Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A sardine in the middle of a bait ball, the spinning silver sphere a sardine shoal makes when it is attacked. On her own she is a finger of silver. But where she swims, the shoal turns tighter and flashes brighter, and a hunter looking at it sees twice as many fish as there are. Every fish beside her counts double, because the hunter can't tell one from two.

She isn't brave. She is in the middle, turning when the others turn, and that is where the trick happens.

## How she got this way

She was in the great run, the winter migration up the coast where the sardines come by the billion and every dolphin, shark, gannet and whale comes with them. She lived through it, and then the next one. Whatever is in the middle of a ball that survives the run, the others crowd round next time.

The card is this. **Count as 2 Fish** is the shoal looking twice its size around her: herself and the fish beside her. It doubles what's counted, not what anyone does. Strength 1 is a sardine.

## What the animals say

The sardines don't name each other, and a named sardine is a strange thing. If anyone names her it's the hunters: the dolphins that circle a ball and slow down because it looks bigger than it should.

## One moment

A ball of sardines the size of a whale turns in blue water. A dolphin swings toward it, sees the size of it, and circles once more before it goes in.

## Character

The Ghost Army of 1944: one small unit with painted tanks that made itself look like a whole army.

## Names

- **Glint** (the dolphins): the flash of silver that makes one fish look like two. One beat, a seen thing; Glint is also one of the English names the naming skill gives for the Slovak Očko's feeling.
- **Shimmer** (the dolphins): the whole ball turning. Softer and longer.

## Stretch

Medium. A bait ball looking bigger than it is is real. A single sardine with a name is the honest stretch: in a shoal there is no individual, and the legend has to make one.

## Open

- The counting applies to everything that counts animals, not only Fish (`docs/rules/rulings.md`, Martin 2026-10-31); the legend reads it as a hunter miscounting.
