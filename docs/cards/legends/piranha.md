# [PIRANHA], the first bite

> Nothing happens until she bites. Then everything does.

Legendary, Jungle, strength 2. Fish. Roar: remove each enemy next to 2 or more of your Fish. Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A red-bellied piranha in a jungle river, small, the size of a hand. On her own she is a fish. Piranhas mostly eat scraps, and most of the time a shoal of them is nervous, not hungry. But when something is caught between the shoal's fish, she is the one who bites first, and when she does, the others do. The water boils, and whatever was in the middle of it isn't there afterwards.

Without the shoal she does nothing. With it, she decides when.

## How she got this way

A dry season shrank her river to a string of pools, and a hundred piranhas were stuck in each one with nothing to eat. She bit first, at the first thing that waded in. She has been the first bite since.

The card is this. **Remove each enemy next to 2 or more of your Fish** is the frenzy: only what is caught between the shoal, and all of it at once. Strength 2 is one small fish who matters only with the others.

## What the animals say

The capybaras and the wading birds have a word for the stretch of river where she lives, and it is her name. The other piranhas don't name her; they follow her.

## One moment

A capybara wades in at the shallow edge. Two dark shapes hang in the brown water on either side of it. A third, smaller one turns once, and then the water is white.

## Character

The one who throws the first stone in a riot: nothing happens until she does, and then it all does.

## Names

- **Nip** (the river's animals): the first bite, small, plain, one beat. Its risk is that it sounds too small and cute.
- **Boil** (the river's animals): what the water does after her. One beat, odd as a name, and it says the frenzy.

## Stretch

Small. The frenzy needing the shoal is the card exactly. The real piranha's frenzy is rarer than its reputation, which is fine: the legend is the reputation.
