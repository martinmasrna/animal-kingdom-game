# [TIGER], the tigress who kills twice

> She lands on one, and takes the next.

Legendary, Jungle, strength 7. Cat. Apex Predator. Roar: remove an adjacent enemy of strength 7 or less. Deck: none yet. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

An old tigress, the queen of the lakes in her forest, who has lost an eye and none of her strength. A tiger lands on its prey and eats it; she lands on one and, before the others can move, takes the next. Two kills on one arrival. Deer, boar, a leopard that stayed too long. She is the oldest tiger anyone in the forest has known, and the most fought: her face carries every fight she has won.

## How she got this way

She took the lakes from her own mother in a fight the forest still talks about, and held them for longer than any tigress before her. The eye went to a crocodile at the lake's edge, the one she killed: a crocodile three times her length. After that, nothing in the water or on the shore stood next to her.

The card is this. **Apex Predator** is her landing on her prey and eating it. **Roar: remove an adjacent enemy of strength 7 or less** is the second kill, the one beside it, anything up to her own size.

## What the animals say

The deer and the langur monkeys give the same alarm call for every tiger, and a special one for her. Her own kind have nothing to do with her except stay out of her ground. The name is the forest's, from the prey, about the eye.

## One moment

A lake at dawn. A tigress lies in the shallows with water on her back, one eye milky, watching the deer on the far shore drink.

## Character

Real: Machli of Ranthambore, the most photographed tiger in the world, who held the lakes for years, lost an eye, killed a 14-foot crocodile, and lived to about twenty. Fiction: Beatrix Kiddo in *Kill Bill*, going through the room. Famous tigers to stay clear of: Shere Khan, Tigger, Tony.

## Names

- **One-Eye** (the prey): on Martin's shelf from the honey badger list, 2026-10-09: "some old, probably large cat that has been scarred with a lot of fights". This is the card it was waiting for, by the shelf's own description.
- **Fishmark** (the prey): what Machli means. She was named for a fish-shaped mark on her face. In English it is a seen mark, a compound, and it carries the real tigress without citing her.

## Stretch

None. A tiger's landing and the second kill beside it are the card; the eye and the crocodile are the legend, taken from a real tigress.

## Open

- Female is my choice, from Machli; the shelf note says "large cat", not male or female.
