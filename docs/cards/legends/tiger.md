# Gorrak One-Eye, the fighter

> A lifetime of fighting and violence, to the point where it became his whole identity and he doesn't know what he is without it.

Legendary, Jungle. Gorrak One-Eye: Apex Predator. Roar: remove an adjacent enemy of strength 7 or less. Name: Gorrak One-Eye (draft, Martin, 2026-11-11: "Gorrak sounds a bit better probably"). No legend is written yet.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

Martin, 2026-11-10: "what I'm going for would be 'warrior' or 'fighter' effect. Someone like Frank Castle maybe, with a lifetime of fighting and violence, to the point where it became his whole identity and he doesn't know what he is without it." One-Eye was his shelf name for "some old, probably large cat that has been scarred with a lot of fights" (2026-10-09).

## The name's shape

- A name and an earned second half (Martin, 2026-11-11: "maybe the issue is that One-Eye as a standalone name just doesn't work that well? maybe it should instead be [NAME] One-Eye, kinda like Logen Ninefingers").
- Two beats before One-Eye, and a harsh sound (Martin, 2026-11-11, on Garron: "G and RR sounds harsh, warrior-like", "still maybe 6/10"; then Gorran "the best of these probably"; then Gorrak "a bit better probably").
