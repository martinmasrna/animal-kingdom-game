# [WHITE WOLF], the one who breaks the trail

> In deep snow the pack walks in her prints.

Legendary, Forest, strength 5. Canine. Once each turn, an ally can roam for free. Decks: Roaming Pack, Hunters. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A white wolf who goes first. In deep snow a wolf pack walks single file, each wolf stepping in the prints of the one in front, and the one in front does the work: she breaks the trail. With her ahead, the others move for nothing. One of them can always go where it needs to without paying for it.

She doesn't lead the hunt. She leads the way to it.

## How she got this way

She was the smallest of her litter in a winter of deep snow, and the only one light enough to stay on top of the crust where the others sank in. She went first because she could, and found the way, and the pack has walked in her prints since.

The card is this. **Once each turn, an ally can roam for free** is the trail she breaks: one of yours moves without spending a move, because she has already done the walking.

## What the animals say

Her pack say her name the way travellers say the name of a guide. The deer see the tracks the next morning and think it was one wolf, because every print is in hers.

## One moment

From high up: a single line of tracks across a white valley, so clean it looks like one animal walked it. Then the line reaches the trees, and eight wolves come out of it.

## Character

Strider leading the hobbits out of Bree: the ranger who knows the way, and the others step where he steps. Famous dog to stay clear of: Balto.

## Stretch

None. Wolves really do walk single file in the leader's prints in deep snow, and a free move is exactly that.
