# [ELEPHANT], the bull who walks where he likes

> You hear him in your feet before you see him.

Legendary, Savanna, strength 9. Hungry 6. Roam. Whenever this lands, remove all adjacent enemies of strength 3 or less. Deck: Roaming Pack. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

An old bull elephant who left the herd long ago, as bulls do, and walks the plain alone. He is the biggest thing anyone has seen. He isn't angry, and he isn't hunting. He is going somewhere, and whatever small thing is in his way when he puts his feet down is not there afterwards. Jackals at a carcass, a nest of guineafowl, a warthog that didn't move: none of it is a fight. He doesn't notice it.

He eats all day and still needs more. When the food runs short he gets smaller and slower, like any animal.

## How he got this way

He was pushed out of his mother's herd at fourteen, the way every young bull is, and joined the bull groups for a while, and then stopped needing them. He has walked his own routes for fifty years since. The small animals learned his routes, more or less.

The card is this. **Roam** is a bull with nowhere he has to be. **Whenever this lands, remove all adjacent enemies of strength 3 or less** is his feet: whatever was small and close when he arrived. **Hungry 6** is what a body that size eats every day.

## What the animals say

The small animals say his name the way people say the name of a storm: it isn't anyone's fault, you just get out of the way. They feel him coming through the ground, the rumble elephants make that you hear in your chest. The lions don't mind him; they keep clear too.

## One moment

Jackals pulling at a carcass in the evening. One lifts its head, ears flat, and all of them are gone in a second. A long time later the grey shape comes over the rise, stops at the carcass, sniffs it, and walks on through where they were.

## Character

Godzilla: not angry at anything; whatever is small enough is just in the way.

## Names

- **Rumble** (the small animals): what they feel in the ground before they see him. A real elephant sound, plain English, and it is a warning.
- **Fluffy** (the small animals, as a joke): from Martin's shelf, "the joke of it on something huge still works". It works only if Martin wants the card to be funny, and it is the opposite of Rumble's register.

## Stretch

Small. Lone old bulls are real, and elephants do trample small animals without meaning to. Roam on an elephant is the wandering. Strength 9 and the sweep fit.

## Open

- Whether Fluffy is for this card or another huge one is Martin's call; the shelf doesn't say.
