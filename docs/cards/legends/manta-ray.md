# [MANTA RAY], the shadow that comes to the reefs at evening

> She only visits reefs that are held.

Legendary, Open Ocean, strength 4. Fish. Dusk: if you control 2 or more regions, draw 2 cards. Decks: Fish, Small Fry. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who she is

A giant manta, wider than a reef fish can see across. At evening she comes in from the open sea to the cleaning stations, the coral heads where small fish pick parasites off the big ones. She glides over them, wings slowly moving, and the cleaners come up to her. She is no danger to anyone; she eats plankton. But she only comes to a reef that is quiet, held, safe. Where the reef is fought over, she doesn't come.

When she comes, she brings the evening with her: the plankton rising, the light going green.

## How she got this way

Mantas remember. A real manta comes back to the same cleaning stations for years, and she has been coming to these for longer than any of the cleaners has been alive. The reefs that kept the peace for her got her visits. The others stopped seeing her.

The card is this. **Dusk** is the evening she comes in. **If you control 2 or more regions** is the reef being held and quiet. **Draw 2 cards** is what her visit brings, and the stretch below.

## What the animals say

The reef fish say her name when the light starts going. To them she is a good sign: if she comes, the reef is all right. The sharks don't care about her one way or the other.

## One moment

Evening at a coral head. The cleaner wrasse stop working on a grouper and wait, all facing the same way. Out of the blue water, a black shape wider than the reef comes slowly in, and stops above them.

## Character

Queen Elizabeth I on progress: she visits only the estates of loyal lords, and her visit makes them.

## Names

- **Big Shade** (the reef fish): the shadow that passes over them, and they are glad of it. Plain, and the reef's own words.
- **Slow Wing** (the reef fish): how she moves. Softer, and closer to the Moon Paw, Shadow Paw shape on Martin's shelf.

## Stretch

Medium. Cleaning stations, the evening visit and returning to the same reefs are real. The draw is the legend's invention: nothing a manta does gives anyone anything.
