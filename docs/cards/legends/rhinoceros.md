# [RHINOCEROS], the one who charges whatever is next to him

> He can't see you. He'll charge anyway.

Legendary, Savanna, strength 8. Hungry 4. Roar: remove an adjacent enemy. Deck: Giants. Name: open.

Internal: a legend is the card's character for naming and art, told from the animals' point of view. No player ever sees this text.

## Who he is

An old black rhino bull, short-sighted and short-tempered. A rhino sees badly past a few body lengths, and a black rhino charges what it can't make out: a bush, a termite mound, a parked truck, a lion. He is the worst of them. Wherever he arrives, the first thing he does is charge whatever is next to him, and it is gone. Then he grazes, and he needs a lot of grazing.

He doesn't want anything. He just doesn't like things being close.

## How he got this way

He was born with his kind's eyes and grew a worse temper than anyone. The lions tried him once when he was young and lost one of theirs; after that nothing on the plain stood next to him on purpose.

The card is this. **Roar: remove an adjacent enemy** is the charge when he arrives: one, the nearest, anything. **Hungry 4** is a big grazer's food. Strength 8 is a rhino.

## What the animals say

The hyenas say his name and look round, because the first sign of him is the huff, the snort before the charge, and after that it's too late. The oxpeckers on his back call it a different thing, rudely, and they're the only ones who can.

## One moment

A lioness lies in the shade of a bush, asleep. A grey shape is grazing on the other side of the bush. It lifts its head, snorts, and goes through the bush.

## Character

The Hulk: he arrives, smashes the nearest thing, and asks nothing.

## Names

- **Squint** (the plain): the seen mark, bad eyes, and it's funny in a hard way. One beat.
- **Old Huff** (the hyenas): the snort that warns you too late. The shelf's "Old [XYZ]" shape.

## Stretch

None worth marking. The charge is the rhino's own nature.
