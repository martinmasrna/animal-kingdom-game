# Cards to look at

Cards Martin flagged from play for a closer look. A card leaves the list once it's been looked at and decided.

- **Skunk** (Aggro, rare, 4): "Return an adjacent enemy to your opponent's hand. They can't play it next turn." Martin's earlier analysis: [`aggro-redesign.md`](aggro-redesign.md) §2.2.3.
- **Sirocco** (Aggro, legendary, 5): "Roar: return all enemy units adjacent to this to their owner's hand." Earlier analysis: [`aggro-redesign.md`](aggro-redesign.md) §2.3.2.
- **Dhole** (Canine, rare, 5): "Whenever another Canine you control enters play, give it +2 strength." (card id `red_wolf`)
- **Viper** (Egg, common, 3): "Roar: an adjacent enemy unit gets -3 strength." Martin: this effect needs to be on a flying unit.
- **Food Aggro redesign** (next design session, alongside Aristocrats). Martin played it against Expert on 2026-10-10: "proper dogshit". His diagnosis: the deck has no way to contest the board, nothing that covers an enemy and stays a big body; Muskrat's conditional removal is the only answer. Some cards also do nothing or have poor numbers. Proposals on the table, untested:
  - A new rare **Hamster** (3, "Has +1 strength for each 10 food you have"): 5 to 8 on turns 3 to 6, when the deck must fight. Takes Dormouse's slot.
  - **Dormouse** ("Dusk: if your hand is empty, gain 10 food") and **Mole** ("gain 5 food; if your hand is empty, draw 2") need an empty hand in a deck that draws constantly (Meerkat, Gopher, Barley, the draw-to-match legendary); Mole is also strictly worse than Squirrel. Cut Dormouse; Mole becomes "Roar: gain 5 food. Draw a card."
  - **Scrooge** (legendary, "gain food equal to the food you gained this turn") is +10 after a Squirrel, Groundhog's common effect on a legendary. Make it double the whole turn or give it a bigger body.
