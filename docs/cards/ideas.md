# Card ideas

Ideas for later sets, nothing settled. Two lists: the expansions with the animals that wait for them, and effects without an animal. A card that ships leaves this file.

## Effects without an animal

### Cards and food

- Flight. Roar: draw a card; if an enemy was removed this turn, draw 2 instead.
- Flight. Roar: draw 2 cards, then put a card from your hand on top of your deck.
- Dawn: draw a random animal of this one's family from your deck.

### Interaction

- Roar: attach to an adjacent enemy. At the start of each of your turns, gain 2 food and it gets -1 strength.
- Flight. Roar: put an adjacent weaker enemy into your hand.

### Strength and movement

- The first time each turn this gains strength, you may move it to an adjacent empty crossroad.
- Roar: give +3 strength to every other copy of this, wherever they are.
- Can be placed next to any of your animals, ignoring connection.

### Copying

- Become a copy of the last common your opponent played.
- Become a copy of the last animal your opponent played (legendary).
- Dawn: your next Roar this turn happens twice (a parrot legendary; the Woodpecker's version then needs a new effect).

### Venom: delayed removal with counterplay

Mark a visible enemy for removal; the opponent gets a turn to cover it, move it or return it.

- Roar: envenom an adjacent enemy.
- Fall: envenom an adjacent enemy.

### Dinosaur Hatch: delayed bodies without food

Protect Eggs, speed up their timers, win with the big bodies they hatch.

- Fragile. Hatch 2: play a random non-legendary Dinosaur from your deck on this crossroad.
- Roar: draw a random Egg from your deck.
- Roar: if an Egg hatched this turn, draw a card.

## Directions (Martin, 2026-10-21/22)

**The small-deck archetype ("your starting deck has no animal of strength 4 or more").** A condition any deck can meet in its own way, so it pulls several lists toward one theme instead of dictating a family's fourteen cards (the Kazakus effect). Target at launch: two commons, two rares, one legendary. The Capybara is the first common, the royal-jelly Bee (`bee_legend_2`) the legendary; the second common and the rares wait for play to show what the deck lacks. A payoff must be flat: an effect that reads strength either rewards big decks or needs no condition at all. Rejected on the way: a cover-protection aura, "+2 to your small animals", deck-to-board placement, "gains its neighbours' strength", "return adjacent 7s", an extra turn. Waiting for Coast: the Seagull, rare, Flight, "Roar: if your starting deck has no animal of strength 4 or more, Scout your opponent's hand."

**Choose-one cards, their own design session.** Skill-rewarding and healthy as long as the options are balanced. A group of animals gets it as its signature, the way Primates own the hand buffs and parrots own repetition. Commons choose between 2, rares 2–3, legendaries 3–4; a handful at each rarity.

**Costlier conditions, a later set.** Singleton, ten different strengths in the starting deck, ten different families. Kazakus is the model, Reno was toxic (Martin).
