# Signature registry

Status: first draft by an Opus agent, 2026-10-05, not yet reviewed by Martin. Fits marked ? are uncertain; Martin's calls override it.

Special animals only: one of a kind, astonishing, widely recognised. Ordinary animals (deer, duck, sparrow, most antelopes and fish) are skins for generic effects and are left out. The signature is what an ordinary nature fan knows the animal for, myths included where everybody believes them (the stork brings babies, a cut earthworm becomes two, a cat has nine lives); trivia is excluded.

Columns: **Shape** is the rule the signature suggests, in this game's terms, without numbers. **Now** says whether that shape exists today: `= Card` (the same effect exists), `~ Card` (close cousin exists), `new`, or for an animal that already has a card, `on card ✓` (its current effect is the fit) / `on card ✗` (its current effect is something else). `?` marks a fit I am unsure of. `—` in Shape means no natural effect: the animal earns a card by its art and a fitting body, not a rule.

## 3. Checks on decisions already made

### Clear mismatches

1. **Chameleon: "can cover animals of any strength."** Nothing about colour change says "beats anything". The chameleon is the world's picture of camouflage, so its rule is Stealth, or, closer to "changes to match what's around it", taking the strength of an adjacent animal. Meanwhile the effect it carries has an obvious owner on the Savanna roster with no effect yet: **Honey Badger**, the small fearless animal that takes on lions. This is the cleanest swap in the pool. (Honey Badger isn't a Cat, so check the aggro deck doesn't need the effect on a particular family; today Chameleon has no tag, so nothing is lost.)
2. **Armadillo: "your adjacent animals have Stealth."** Armor fits; hiding its neighbours does not. Nothing about an armadillo shelters others. The grant belongs to **Zebra** (the herd's stripes stop a predator singling one out), which is on the Savanna roster unassigned.
3. **Naked Mole-Rat: "play another animal next to this."** It passes one direction at best ("never lives alone") and fails the other: nobody hears "play another animal" and thinks of a naked mole-rat. Its real signature, the mammal that lives like an ant colony with a queen, is left unspent; a Rodent that also counts as Colony would be the delight. The animals that do say "brings another one" are **Kangaroo** (Australia, later) and **Stork** (Meadow, launch: the stork brings babies), but both are big, and the size rule wants this effect on something small. No small animal in the launch says "brings another" outright. Martin's call: keep it on the mole-rat as a defensible compromise, or move it to Stork (with "never onto a den") and give the mole-rat its colony identity.
4. **Stray Cat: "if you control another Cat, play another Cat."** A cat's signature is nine lives. The "play another Cat" glue fits the lion's pride (the only social cat), not a lone street cat. Same for **Stray Dog** "play another Canine": a dog fetches. Both glue cards could keep their effects if the family needs them; the point is that "nine lives" and "fetch" are signatures waiting for these two animals.
5. **Fox: "whenever this gains strength, draw a card."** No link to the sly fox. Low stakes (Fox is common in the mind, not astonishing), but stealing or a trick would fit.

### Acceptable but not the best

- **Octopus: "next turn, take 1 additional action."** Passes: eight arms working at once is an obvious read, and no animal fits an extra action better (Hummingbird's speed is weaker). Cost: the octopus also owns ink, camouflage and escape, and the most remarkable ocean animal gets only one card. Ink escape goes to Squid, camouflage to Chameleon, so nothing is wasted. Keep.
- **Sea Otter: "+5 strength while next to another Sea Otter."** Good fit, obvious once said. Holding hands is about not being separated, so "can't be removed while next to another Sea Otter" is the more literal rule; the strength version gets most of it (hard to cover). Either way, keep the pair.
- **Leopard (Snow Leopard's equal-strength cover):** no natural owner exists for that effect, so the leopard is as good as any. Fine.
- **Serval (remove strength 6+):** the natural owner is **Piranha** (small swarm kills big), on the Jungle roster unassigned, but Serval is a Cat and the Cats deck may need the effect on a cat. Family need decides.
- **Python (+1 per removed animal):** a scavenger shape (Vulture, Hyena). Python's "fat after a meal" makes it passable.
- **Ember (golden pheasant), returns when removed:** the phoenix reading holds for a legendary. The everyday survivor is **Cockroach** (City, unassigned), which should get the same shape on a common.
- **Oxpecker (food per big animal in deck):** related to big animals, but the obvious rule is the hitchhiker (free placement beside a big ally).
- **Grizzly Bear (delayed random removal):** no fit; bears say "big" and "hibernate", and Black Bear already has the hibernation delay.
- **Cheetah (den draw):** fine; a sprint reach would be truer, so keep reach unspent for it.
- **Gale (albatross, draws per animal by the den), Fathom (octopus, Scout a legendary), Lynx, Caracal, Groundhog, Gopher:** no real fit, but these are legendaries or ordinary animals. Low stakes.
