# Habitats

Every card belongs to one habitat: its animal's real, well-known home. The launch is six habitats; every other habitat is an expansion, and an animal whose home is outside the launch six waits for it ("we're not pretending that Polar Bear is a forest animal", Martin, 2026-10-27). Within the launch six, any animal fits (Martin, 2026-10-27). The animals waiting for each expansion are in [`../cards/ideas.md`](../cards/ideas.md); his habitat calls are logged in [`log_habitats.md`](log_habitats.md).

## Launch

- **Savanna:** African grassland, gold and brown.
- **Jungle:** tropical rainforest, deep and wet green.
- **Forest:** temperate woods, the bamboo forests of the pandas among them.
- **Meadow:** open fields and hedges, bright and flowering.
- **City:** streets, parks and houses, grey.
- **Open Ocean:** far from shore, sunlit blue water and the seafloor beneath it.

## Expansions

The waters (Martin, 2026-10-28):

- **Deep Ocean:** below where sunlight reaches.
- **Coral Reef:** warm, shallow reefs.
- **Coast:** the shore itself, beaches, rocks, cliffs and tide pools, and the animals living on it: crabs, seagulls, puffins (Martin, 2026-10-28).
- **River and Lake:** fresh water, with the swamps.

The lands:

- **Australia.**
- **Islands:** animals that evolved on islands, in one set: Galápagos, Madagascar, New Zealand, Komodo, and the dodo.
- **Mountains.**
- **Polar:** the ice and snow of both poles, the snowy north and the polar seas.
- **Desert.**
- **Prehistoric:** dinosaurs, pterosaurs and the ancient seas.
- **Ice Age.**

A set theme can cross habitats (Back from the Brink: animals saved from extinction), but it is never a card's habitat: its animals live somewhere real.

## Open

- **The Platypus:** Australia or River and Lake, whichever a player pictures.
- **Grassland outside Africa,** the prairie and the steppe (bison, prairie dog, pronghorn, burrowing owl, saiga, manul): no home yet.
