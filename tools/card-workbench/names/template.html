Draft rules, from the names that already work (Gale, Eon, Aurum, Fathom, Vesper):
  1. One or two words: a name, not a description.
  2. It evokes the card's effect or the animal's essence.
  3. Myth and folklore may be evoked, never cited: no specific work, character or real individual.
  4. Titles only where the animal really has royalty (the lion pride, the colony queens).
  5. Unique in sound across the pool.
  6. No pun that needs explaining.
