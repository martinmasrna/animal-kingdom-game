Legend Names

Connecting…
The rule, from the names you kept or picked (Prince Leo, King Theron, Lord Bumbleton, Ember, Eon, Aurum, Pestis, Brutus, Methuselah, Silverback, Greywhisker, Black Swan): the name is the card's idea, in a word any player can decode. Ember returns from the ashes, Eon loops forever, Aurum is gold, Methuselah is old, Silverback leads, Greywhisker is an old rat, Brutus is a brute. Three shapes carry it:
  1. A meaning word, or a Latin or English root everyone half-knows (Ember, Eon, Aurum, Pestis, Brutus, Methuselah).
  2. A two-part compound of what the animal looks like or does (Silverback, Greywhisker).
  3. A title on a name, grand for royal animals (King Theron), playful for the rest (Lord Bumbleton).
What failed: coined sounds with no meaning (Tocca, Rasca), and words whose meaning doesn't reach the animal (Vesper, Clarion, Sirocco, Gale).
