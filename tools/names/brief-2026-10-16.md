# Fourteen legendary cards that need a name

Animal Kingdom is a two-player tactical card game with real animals. Commons and rares carry plain species names (Lion, Zebra). A legendary is one named individual animal. Names already taken in the game: Prince Leo, Princess Lea, King Theron, Queen Adira, Greywhisker, Black Swan, Big Gus, Lord Bumbleton, Aurum, Iris, Yuki, Pestis, Eon, Vesper, Lobo, Scarlett, Roach King, Luna, Rue, Aya, Calypso, Cuco, Fathom, Arne, Arna, Redbeak, Stonetusk, Ember, Gale, Barley, Mocha, Brutus, Scrooge, Methuselah, Silverback, Queen Marabunta, Queen Honoria, Cairn, Borealis, Clarion, Sirocco.

Strength runs from 1 to 10; most animals are 2 to 7, and 8 or more is the top of the field. "Roar" is an effect on play; "Dawn" and "Dusk" trigger at the start and end of the owner's turn.

1. wolf_legend. Grey wolf, Forest, strength 4. "Roam. Dusk: this gains +1 strength for each adjacent Canine." Art: a big silver-grey alpha with a dark saddle and a scarred muzzle, standing over a beaten rival he has let go, his pack around him, storm light breaking over a green valley. The designer: "the undisputed leader, despite maybe not being the strongest physically. Everyone respects him. He gets stronger from the pack around him and leads by example from the inside." Luna (a white she-wolf) and Lobo (a black wolf) are in the same deck.
2. macaque_legend. Rhesus macaque, City, strength 2. "Roar: draw cards until you have as many as your opponent." Art: a huge old golden-maned macaque sprawled on a hoard of stolen fruit on top of an ancient overgrown stone gateway at sunset, biting a mango. The designer: "a crime boss, very cunning and clever. The cunning one that knows the tricks and knows how to steal the food. Not greedy or gluttonous."
3. honey_badger_legend. Honey badger, Savanna, strength 5. "Armor. Stealth. Poison. Spikes." (It cannot be removed by effects, cannot be targeted, poisons what touches it, and hurts what covers it.) Art: a low, white-backed honey badger walking straight out of the dark toward the viewer, red eyes. The designer: "he just wants to be left alone, minding his own business, eating. But everyone keeps provoking him and he destroys them all. Literally unkillable. Not a protector. Like John Wick, or Snow from the Love, Death and Robots episode Snow in the Desert: an albino who lives alone in a desert, regenerates, and is hunted for what he is."
4. leopard_legend. Leopard, Savanna, strength 8. "Apex Predator. Roam. Reach 2." (Eats weaker animals, moves freely, strikes from two spaces away.) Art: a leopard stalking out of burning night grassland toward the viewer. The designer: "the bogeyman of the herds. When zebra mothers want to scare their foals, they tell the legend of this guy who will come and get them at night if they wander off. Fearless hunter, fearless killer; once he decides to hunt you, he will. Like the Predator: a legend that turns out to be real."
5. tuna_legend. Bluefin tuna, Open Ocean, strength 5. "Roar: for each region you control, draw a card and gain 5 food." Art: a huge silver bluefin cutting through a purple sea under lightning. Feeling: raw speed and power, the ocean's bullet, the traveller that crosses whole oceans.
6. manta_ray_legend. Manta ray, Open Ocean, strength 4. "Dusk: if you control 2 or more regions, draw 2 cards." Art: a manta gliding through dark water, its body speckled with glowing blue light like a constellation, a star field around it. Feeling: the night sky swimming; quiet wonder.
7. piranha_legend. Red-bellied piranha, Jungle river, strength 3. "Your Fish can cover enemies of strength up to the number of your Fish." (The shoal takes down bigger prey together.) Art: a piranha, jaws open, at the front of a dark shoal. Feeling: swarm terror, the one at the front of the horde.
8. raven_legend. Raven, Forest, strength 3. "Flight. Roar: put an animal from your opponent's Remove Pile into your hand." (It takes what the enemy lost.) Art: a black raven calling from a skull on a snowy rock, a red sunset storm, other birds in the sky. Feeling: omen, the one that feeds on the dead.
9. baboon_legend. Baboon, Savanna, strength 3. "Dawn: give 2 random animals in your hand +1 strength." Art: a massive grey-maned old baboon sitting on a rock above a misty valley at dusk, a crescent moon, a Kilimanjaro-like mountain. Feeling: an old cunning boss watching over his land.
10. butterfly_legend. Monarch butterfly, Meadow, strength 0. "When this gains strength in your hand, evolve." (It starts as an egg in the hand and grows through caterpillar and chrysalis into the Monarch.) Art: a glowing backlit monarch on a dry thistle in golden light. Feeling: tender wonder, the little thing that becomes beautiful.
11. wildebeest_legend. Wildebeest, Savanna, strength 5. "Your regions produce 5 more food." Art: a wildebeest on green plains under a rainbow, the herds at a river. Feeling: plenty, the good season of the herds, the great migration.
12. zebra_legend. Zebra, Savanna, strength 4. "Roar: draw a card for each different Hoofed animal you control." Art: a zebra on a rock under the Milky Way, the herds at the waterhole below. Feeling: the bright one the herds gather around.
13. giraffe_legend. Giraffe, Savanna, strength 7. "Your opponent's hand is revealed." Art: a giraffe's head rising above a sea of clouds at sunrise. Feeling: serene, the one who sees everything.
14. locust. Desert locust, Meadow, strength 2. "Roar: remove all other animals and discard your hand." (The swarm eats everything, yours included.) Art: a golden locust on a dead stalk under a black swarm sky over barren earth. Feeling: apocalypse, the plague that eats the world. The designer liked the direction of "Yellow Death" and "First of Many" but neither was quite it: they read as an event or a title, not an animal's name.
