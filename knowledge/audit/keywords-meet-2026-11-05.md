# Keyword audit: where the keywords meet (2026-11-05)

How the keywords of `docs/rules/keywords.md` behave where they meet each other and each kind of removal (a Roar that
removes, chosen, mass, random or remove-all; an Apex Predator eating; a trap: Spikes, the Boar, the Hippopotamus, the
Golden Orb-Weaver; a Fall; a Poison bite; a Locust's eat; a Dawn removal; a plague; a bounce; a cover). Each meeting is
one test in `animal_kingdom/tests/audit/test_keywords_meet.py`, set up with real cards and asserted from the
definitions (keywords.md, overview.md, rulings.md), the code read only afterwards to find the setup.

Counts: 174 meetings; 173 ok; 0 engine fixed; 1 two readings; 0 engine wrong and unfixed. No engine change, so
nothing to mirror in `engine_rs/src`.

Worth knowing, all as defined and all ok:
- An Earthworm removed next to a Hippopotamus is a card and two more: the Worms it places land next to the Hippo too,
  are removed (a placed animal is seen by the traps beside it, rulings.md "Place" versus "play") and each falls.
- The Locusts are no one's, so they are an enemy cover even of the caster's own animals: the caster's Gazelle flees
  from its own swarm and the caster's Hedgehog's Spikes remove the Locust on it.
- A Poison coverer that walks off by roaming still dies at the poisoner's next turn (the poison is the coverer's); one
  its owner returns to hand first is spared, and is a fresh animal when played again.
- A doubled Coyote ("It roams") moves an ally twice.
- When the caster's own Toad is under the caster's own Locust, the Toad's Poison resolves before the Locust's eat
  (the Toad was played first, the order keywords.md gives for delayed effects), so the Toad survives.
