# Den captures available within one turn, mined from the human games (2026-11-05)

The raw material for a daily puzzle: every position in Martin's recorded games where the player about to act could capture the enemy den in at most two actions. Analysis only. Nothing in the game, engine, bots or cards changed. Tool: `tools/puzzles/mine.py`. Output: `tools/puzzles/forced_captures-2026-11-05.jsonl`, one line per position, with the counts in `forced_captures-2026-11-05.summary.json`. The run took 10 minutes on 4 cores, with no sampling; three full runs gave the same 154 positions.

## What was searched

- **Games.** All 2,437 games (1,482 files) under `results/human_games/`, on branch `card-balance`. Old card ids were first mapped to their current ids with the web replayer's own map (`web/renamed.py`). **302 games replayed. 2,135 were skipped:** 1,631 hit an EngineError partway through (a logged action is no longer legal, because a card changed since), 322 hit a KeyError (a card that no longer exists), and 182 could not be set up (a retired premade deck slug, or a missing card). The skipped games are mostly the older ones. A log that diverges is skipped whole; positions before the divergence point are not used.
- **Positions.** **2,848**: every moment a player was about to take the first action of a turn (nothing resolving, no action yet taken). 1,442 belong to a human seat, 1,406 to a bot (1,397 Expert, 4 Normal, 5 Easy).
- **Lines.** At most two turn actions (draw, place, roam). Every sub-choice the engine asks for along the way is expanded, and so is every placement an effect grants while it resolves (Mole, Remora, Impala, Rabbit, Prince Leo and Princess Lea). Those are free, and the output marks them `"sub": true`. A line counts when `rules.is_terminal` reports the actor winning by `hq_capture`.
- **Hidden information.** Only the actor's own seat is used. The search runs on a world re-sampled from the actor's view (`bots.determinize`: their own deck reshuffled, the opponent's hand and deck re-dealt). Every line found is then replayed on 4 more independently sampled worlds and kept only if it captures in all of them. So a line that needs a lucky draw is dropped, unless every sampled draw works. This is a sample of 5 worlds, not a proof over every possible draw. A choice the opponent makes in the middle of the line would have to work for every option the opponent has, but none of the 154 lines contains one.
- **Bounds.** Both actions are enumerated in full. **131 positions** were too wide for the cap of 20,000 nodes. Those were searched again with the second action restricted to den captures, so a line whose second action is an ordinary placement that captures through its effect could be missing there. 20 of the 154 positions found came from that fallback (`search_complete: false`). In 7 positions the engine raised an error inside the search; they count as having no capture.
- **Check against the true game.** Every line was also replayed on the real game state. 153 of 154 capture there too. The one exception (`web_20261030T213040_7K2LNQ.jsonl` line 10, action 10) has a choice that names a unit by its instance id (Princess Lea fetching Prince Leo from the deck). That id differs between the sampled worlds and the real game, so the miss comes from how the choice is labelled, not from hidden information. The player did capture there.

- Humans always took a one-action capture: 13 times by capturing, and the 2 others were turns they won on food anyway. Humans missed **21 of 83** two-action captures (25%), counting only turns they did not win some other way.
- The Expert took 50 of 55 two-action captures. One of the 5 was not a miss: the opponent conceded after the Expert's first action (`77LLTQ` line 1). The other **4 are real Expert misses** (puzzles 3, 4 and 10 below, and `OXHIXA` line 10, action 56). That is a bot finding, not a card signal: it belongs to the `bot-change` skill, where the `_den_openers` dial already exists for RefereeBot. Nothing was changed here.
- The 1-action count includes lines where one placement grants free placements that end on the den (a Mole or Princess Lea chain).
- Distinct capturing lines per position, at the shortest length: 19 positions have exactly one, 37 have two, 19 have three, and 35 have ten or more. A position with one line or a few makes the better puzzle.

## The first ten puzzles

The map is the 5×3 grid map_b. Crossroads are "rank,lane": rank 1 is in front of A's den, rank 5 in front of B's. Each is a two-action line, chosen for a unique or near-unique solution, a player (or the Expert) who missed it, and a different idea each time. Ordered from the one to show first.

1. **The stranded Elephant.** `results/human_games/web/web_20261031T131750_ILXWMW.jsonl` line 4, action 46 (A, human, turn 18; unique line). A's hand is empty. A's legendary Elephant (9, Roam) already stands on 5,3 in front of B's den, but nothing connects it, because 4,3 is empty, and A's Bush Dog is on 3,3. Line: roam the Bush Dog 3,3→4,3, then roam the Elephant onto B's den. It is hard to see because the hand is empty and the Elephant looks finished. The missing piece is the connection, and the Bush Dog's Pup, left on 3,3 when it roams, keeps the chain whole. The player drew and placed an African Wild Dog on 4,3. That connected the Elephant too, but used up the turn.
2. **Predator first, Sloth last.** `web_20261017T100008_DRXFAW.jsonl` line 0, action 17 (A, human, turn 8; unique). A's line runs up the middle lane to a Blue Whale (12) on 4,2. B's Oxpecker (2) holds 5,2 and a Sperm Whale legend (8) holds 5,3. Hand: Crocodile, Blue Whale, Sloth. Line: Crocodile onto the Oxpecker, then Sloth onto the den. It is hard because the Sloth (3) can also cover the Oxpecker, but then the Crocodile, an Apex Predator, cannot land on the empty den. The predator has to do the clearing and the weak card takes the den. The player spent both actions on the Titan Blue Whale.

## Caveats

- 88% of the games could not be replayed on the current cards, so these numbers describe the 302 games played on cards close to today's. The human and bot rates come from small samples (83 and 55 two-action positions).
- A puzzle built from one of these positions should be checked by hand against today's card text before it is published. The cards are still moving on this branch.
- "Missed" is narrow. The player did not take the den that turn and did not win that turn any other way. Most humans who missed still won the game.
