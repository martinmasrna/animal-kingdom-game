# Building an invented animal world: research of 2026-11-19

Three Sonnet web-research passes after Martin decided the animals live in a world of our own (`docs/design/world.md`). A delta on `world-first-study-2026-10-24.md`, which was written for real animals; read that first for the per-society generator (position, bargain, verb and weakness, namer, one event). Most sources are secondary; what could not be verified is said.

## How worlds are structured

- Card games hang mechanics on one axis and let the others be flavour. Legends of Runeterra: the region is the faction, it "define[s] what a champion does and what other cards from that region tend to do" (Andrew Yip, [Geek Culture](https://geekculture.co/geek-interview-building-decks-in-legends-of-runeterra-from-the-ground-up-with-design-director-andrew-yip)); 6 regions and 24 champions at launch, capped at 10 regions later; species sit inside regions. Magic: colour carries mechanics, creature type is a skin; Ravnica's ten guilds are the ten colour pairs and races cross guilds ([Guild Order](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/guild-order-part-1-2018-09-10)). Root: a faction is a way of leading and playing; species is flavour ([Leder diary](https://ledergames.com/blogs/news/root-designer-diary-2-rats-let-s-get-rowdy)).
- Kind as the faction failed: Rosewater on Bloomburrow, "Limiting each archetype to one animal was too limiting", "Factions should have been about something that could group together numerous animals", and its draft was "on rails" ([State of Design 2025](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/state-of-design-2025), via a fetch summary; quote the page before reusing). Most animal types had too few cards; an all-animals grouping was cut for endless upkeep and arguments over membership ([Bloomburrow handoff](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/bloomburrow-vision-design-handoff-part-1)).
- Growth: Runeterra launched a small cast on a broad map with unclaimed land between regions; a region needs a minimum cast (the Void was doubted with eight champions); Ravnica fixed its container (ten guilds) first and released five and five.

## Inventing creatures from real animals

- The method agrees across Pokémon, Monster Hunter, Avatar's Pandora and Avatar: The Last Airbender: keep the real body plan and head as anchors, change one region of the body, justify the change by function or habitat. Monster Hunter kept Nergigante for its silhouette ([Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2017/11/17/monster-hunter-world-bestiary-day-3-the-destructive-titan-nergigante)); Appa is a bison's head on a manatee's body; Pikachu's species was loose, its one colour carried the one idea. No source gives a count of features that can change.
- Familiar plus novel: Hekkert, Snelders and van Wieringen (2003) found typicality and novelty predict preference about equally and suppress each other ([abstract](https://research.vu.nl/en/publications/most-advanced-yet-acceptable-typicality-and-novelty-as-joint-pred/)); consumer products, no effect sizes; "one twist" is a heuristic consistent with it, not a measured optimum.
- Family identity at small size: few, clearly separated codes are learnable (warning-colour learning in a game study); downward-pointing angles read as threat, round as warm (lab studies, mixed). Bloomburrow chose "a more animalistic design" and put species identity in gear and clothing ([concept art](https://magicuntapped.com/index.php/articles/the-strange-world-of-bloomburrow-concept-art)).

## A world's one idea and its tone

- In the best-sourced cases the one idea is a feeling the audience leaves with (Totoro: "pleasant, glad feelings"; Pokémon's collecting; Kaladesh's topline "optimistic steampunk", [Making Kaladesh](https://magic.wizards.com/en/articles/archive/magic-story/making-kaladesh-2016-11-09)) or a value held under pressure (Mononoke's proposal: "even in the middle of hatred and killings, there are things worth living for", [translation](https://www.nausicaa.net/wiki/Princess_Mononoke_(project_proposal))). Magic gives each plane a two-phrase topline; Runeterra had no premise and was retrofitted; Le Guin found who her dragons were decades after the first book ([her site](https://www.ursulakleguin.com/the-books-of-earthsea)).
- Warm worlds get conflict from something loved at stake, not from villains (Mononoke, Spiritfarer: death "not portrayed as a cozy concept", the care around it is). Inference from the cases.
- Twist: a stereotype is a type with one trait; the twist keeps the seed and adds a motive or history that reframes it (Le Guin's dragons kin to humans who chose freedom). Sanderson: "Expand what you already have before you add something new"; cultures "identical, except for one little defining trait" is the failure ([essay](https://brandonsanderson.com/sandersons-third-law-of-magic/)).
