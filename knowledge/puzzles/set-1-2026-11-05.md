# Puzzle set 1 (2026-11-05)

The first puzzles: two from the mined den captures (`knowledge/audit/forced-captures-2026-11-05.md`) and twenty composed
by hand around one idea each, where the obvious move walks past the idea. Every file passes
`tools/puzzles/solve.py check`: a legal position with no chance in it, the den forced against every defence, one winning
first turn. The boards read with the solver's den (A, "+") at the bottom and the defender's (B, "-") at the top, strength
after each name, a stack bottom to top. "Free" marks a placement a Roar gave. The defence's reply is the one the server
plays: the one that leaves the solver the fewest ways to finish.

## The river horse (`river_horse`)

In 1 turn · Traps · difficulty 1 of 5

```
            [B's den]
5  .                      | -Hippopotamus 8        | -Grizzly Bear 8       
4  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
3  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
2  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
1  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
            [A's den]
   lanes: 1 (left) | 2 | 3 (right); a stack reads bottom / ... / top
A's hand: Rabbit 3, Mouse 4
B's hand: Lion 7
Food: A 0, B 0
```

**The obvious move:** Rabbit first, onto 4,1, so its Bunny can step up: the Bunny lands on 5,1 beside the Hippo, which removes it, and the den is out of reach.

**The solution:** Mouse on 4,1; Rabbit on 5,1; (free) Bunny into the den.


**The idea:** The Hippo removes a weak animal placed next to it, but the Rabbit's Roar comes first: its Bunny is in the den before the Hippo answers.

**Hint:** A Roar happens before anything beside it can answer.

```
   lanes: 1 (left) | 2 | 3 (right); a stack reads bottom / ... / top
A's hand: Honey Badger 3, Black Mamba 4, Lion 7
B's hand: Lion 7
Food: A 0, B 0
```

**The obvious move:** The Black Mamba, the removal card: it can't touch a Grizzly (8), and the Lion can't cover one.

**The solution:** Honey Badger on 2,2; its Roar picks the Grizzly Bear on 3,2; Black Mamba into the den.


**The idea:** Removing the Grizzly brings back your own buried Lion, which joins your cut-off Lions to your den; only the Honey Badger removes something that strong.

**Hint:** Your Lion under the Grizzly is not gone.

## Thorns (`thorns`)

In 2 turns · Spikes · difficulty 3 of 5

```
            [B's den]
5  -Hedgehog 5            | -Grizzly Bear 8        | -Grizzly Bear 8       
4  .                      | +Lion 7                | -Grizzly Bear 8       
3  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
2  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
1  .                      | +Lion 7                | .                     
            [A's den]
   lanes: 1 (left) | 2 | 3 (right); a stack reads bottom / ... / top
A's hand: Skunk 4, Cheetah 6, Pup 1
B's hand: Pup 1, Stick Insect 6
Food: A 0, B 0
```

**The obvious move:** The Cheetah onto the Hedgehog, the one den-front animal it can cover: the Spikes remove it. And with the Skunk on your own Lion at 4,2 (to send the Grizzly home), the Stick Insect covers the Skunk and cuts the chain.

**The solution:** Skunk on 4,1; its Roar picks the Hedgehog on 5,1; Cheetah on 5,1.
 The defence's best reply: Pup on 3,3; Stick Insect on 2,3.
 Then: Pup into the den.


**The idea:** The Cheetah dies on the Hedgehog's Spikes; the Skunk sends the Hedgehog home instead, where it can't be played next turn, and the Cheetah must fill the gap at once, before the Stick Insect does (and the Skunk must not stand on your own Lion, where the Stick Insect could cover it and cut you off).

**Hint:** An animal sent back to its owner's hand by a Skunk can't be played on their next turn.
