Key Pieces

  • No life. You win if you control Key A, Key B and Key C at the start of your turn. You lose if you must draw from an empty deck.
  • Each turn: your mana goes up by 1 (up to 10) and refills, your units become ready, you draw 1.
  • Play cards by paying their cost. Up to 6 pieces a side, Keys included. A unit cannot attack the turn it is played. Keys never attack.
  • Each ready unit may attack an enemy unit or Key once: both deal their attack to each other (a Key deals 0). Damage stays.
  • Guard: while the defender has a Guard, attacks must target a Guard. Hits ignore Guard.
  • The first player starts with 3 cards; the second with 4 and the Coin (gain 1 mana this turn).
