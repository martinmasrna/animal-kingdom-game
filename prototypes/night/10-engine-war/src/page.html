Engine War

Points come only from Posts on your table: at the start of your turn each gives its yield (green). 20 points at the start of your turn wins.

Your turn: your Posts yield; your whole hand plays itself (gold adds coin, red adds damage, a Post goes onto your table, Draw adds 1 coin and plays the next 2 cards of your deck). Then:

  • Buy row cards with coin; they go to your discard pile and the row refills.
  • Shoot enemy Posts: one whose health (red) your damage covers is destroyed. While a Wall Post (striped) stands, only it can be shot.

Unspent coin and damage are lost. You draw 5 for your next turn, shuffling your discard pile when the deck runs out. Trash removes another card in your hand or discard pile from the game.

You start with 7 Coin and 3 Shot; the first player's first hand is 3 cards. When the market deck runs out, the round is finished and the higher score wins.
