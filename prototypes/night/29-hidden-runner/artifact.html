Hidden runner

One side is the Runner (◆, hidden), the other the Hunter (two pawns). Roles swap every game.

The Runner starts on any node of the top row, unseen. Each round both sides gain 2 energy (max 5) and draw a card. Then:

Runner: a free Step (move 1 or stay) or one card. The card's name is announced, never where it went. The Runner can never enter a pawn's node or a Block.

Hunter: move each pawn 1 (or leave it) and play at most one card, before or after moving. Tap a pawn, then a node. End the round when done.

A pawn landing on the Runner wins for the Hunter. The Runner is shown at the end of rounds 3, 6 and 9 (marked on the round track). The Runner wins by surviving round 12.

Search: name a node; you learn whether the Runner is on it or next to it (✓ or ✕). Hide makes this round's Searches answer ✕.
