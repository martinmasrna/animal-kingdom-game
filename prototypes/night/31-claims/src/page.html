Claims

Both decks hold the same 24 cards: three of each card on the list. Life 20. Mana goes up by 1 each turn (max 10). Draw 1 each turn; an empty deck costs 1 life, then 2, then 3.

Play any card face-down and name any card on the list, paying the named card's cost. The other player may challenge:

  • Not challenged: it works as the named card for the rest of the game.
  • Challenged and true: the challenger loses 3 life, the card works.
  • Challenged and false: the card is discarded with no effect, the mana stays spent, the claimer loses 3 life.

Units attack from the turn after they are played: an enemy unit (both deal their attack to each other; damage stays) or the enemy player. Up to 5 units a side. Hit 3 and Blast deal 3 and 5 damage to a unit or player; Draw 2 draws 2 cards.

Every card turns face-up when it leaves play, so discard piles show the truth. The second player starts with one more card and the Coin (+1 mana once).

Reduce the other player to 0 life.
