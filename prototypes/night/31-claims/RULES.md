# 31 Claims

A unit duel where every card is played face-down as a claimed card, and the opponent may call the bluff. Every number is a placeholder.

## Setup

- Both decks are the same known list: 8 cards, 3 copies each (24). Life 20. Up to 5 units a side.
- Random first player. The first player draws 3, the second draws 4 and holds the Coin (once: +1 mana this turn; never claimed or challenged).
- Discard piles are face-up.

## Turn

1. Maximum mana +1 (cap 10), refill. Your units become ready. Draw 1; drawing from an empty deck loses 1 life, then 2, then 3.
2. **Play by claim**, any number of times: put a card from hand face-down, name any card on the list, pay the named card's cost.
3. The opponent may **challenge** at once.
   - Not challenged: the card works as the named card. A unit enters face-down with the named stats and is that card for the rest of the game; a spell resolves as named.
   - Challenged and true: turned face-up, the challenger loses 3 life, the card resolves.
   - Challenged and false: turned face-up and discarded with no effect, the mana stays spent, the claimer loses 3 life.
4. Each ready unit not played this turn may attack an enemy unit (both deal their attack to each other; damage stays) or the enemy player.
5. End the turn.

A face-down card turns face-up when it leaves play (destroyed unit, resolved spell), so the true card always ends up in the discard pile.

## End

Life 0 or less loses; both at once is a draw.
