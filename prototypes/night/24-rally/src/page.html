Rally

Each shot card has a Pace, a Reach and a stamina cost. Both players use the same 20-card deck and hold 5 cards.

A point. The server plays any shot. Then the players take turns: each must answer the last shot with a card whose Reach is at least that shot's Pace, paying its cost. A player who cannot, or lets it go, loses the point. Afterwards both discard the shots they played and draw back up to 5.

Stamina is 10 each at the start of every game and carries from point to point within the game.

First to 4 points wins a game; first to 3 games wins the match. The serve passes to the other player after every point.
