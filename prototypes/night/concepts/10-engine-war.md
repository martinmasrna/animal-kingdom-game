# 10 Engine war

Every number here is a placeholder. Borrowed: a face-up market row of five that refills when a card is bought, as in Star Realms' trade row. The rest is not that game: there is no life total and points come only from Posts on the table.

**The question.** When every point comes from engines on the table that the opponent can shoot, does the choice between buying engines and buying guns stay a real one?

## Setup

- Each player's starting deck: Coin ×7 (+1 coin), Shot ×3 (+1 damage). Shuffle.
- The market deck (36 cards, list below) is shuffled; 5 cards lie face-up as the row.
- Random first player. The first player's first hand is 3 cards; every other hand is 5.
- Points start at 0. Target: 20.

## Turn

1. **Yield.** Each of your Posts on the table gives you its Yield in points. If you now have 20 or more, you win.
2. **Play** your whole hand. Coin and damage add to this turn's pools; a Post goes onto the table and stays there until destroyed; other effects resolve.
3. **Buy** any number of row cards with coin; each goes to your discard pile, and the row refills from the market deck at once.
4. **Shoot.** Spend damage on enemy Posts: a Post is destroyed when you assign damage at least equal to its Health this turn (damage does not carry over between turns). While the opponent has a Wall Post on the table, their other Posts cannot be targeted. Destroyed Posts go to their owner's discard pile.
5. Discard your played cards (not Posts on the table) and any leftover cards, then draw 5, shuffling your discard into a new deck when needed.

Unspent coin and damage are lost.

## End

- 20 points at the start of your turn wins.
- When the market deck runs out, the round is finished (both players have had the same number of turns) and the higher score wins; equal is a draw.

## Builder notes

- Greedy: buy the most expensive affordable card, repeat until it can buy nothing; spend damage on the highest-Yield Post it can destroy.
- Racer: never buys Shots and never shoots; buys Posts first, then Coin 2.
- Set positions: denial (the opponent's Big Post will take them to 20 next turn and you hold 7 damage: destroy it); don't waste (6 damage against two Health-5 Posts: destroy one, don't split); don't waste coin (8 coin and a Big Post in the row: don't end the buy phase with nothing).
