# 12 Deck is life

Every number here is a placeholder.

**The question.** When your deck is your life (damage burns cards off its top, drawing extra costs life, recycling your discard heals), do drawing and recycling become real decisions?

## Setup

- Each player has the same 30-card deck, shuffled. There is no life total: your deck is your life. Board max 6 units.
- Random first player; the first player draws 3, the second draws 4 and gets the Coin (0 mana: gain 1 mana this turn).
- Deck sizes are always visible to both players.

## Turn

1. Your maximum mana goes up by 1 (cap 10) and refills. Your units become ready. Draw 1.
2. Play cards by paying their cost. A unit cannot attack on the turn it is played.
3. Each ready unit may attack once: an enemy unit (both deal their attack to each other; damage stays) or the enemy player.
4. End the turn.

Damage to a player banishes that many cards from the top of their deck: they are turned face-up into a banished pile, out of the game for good. Destroyed units and used spells go to their owner's discard pile, which is face-up and is not reshuffled.

## End

A player who must draw (the turn's draw or any card's draw) from an empty deck loses at once.

## Builder notes

- Greedy: plays the most expensive affordable cards (Draw 2 only while its deck holds 3 or more cards, Recover whenever it has a discard); each ready unit attacks an enemy unit it destroys and survives, otherwise the enemy player.
- Racer: every attack and every Burn at the enemy player.
- Set positions: lethal (the opponent's deck holds 3 cards and your ready Raider can attack: attack the player); don't kill yourself (your deck holds 1 card: never play Draw 2); free trade (your ready Line against an enemy Raider: attack it).
