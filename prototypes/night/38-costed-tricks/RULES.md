# Costed tricks

Trick-taking where a card played face-up costs energy by its rank, and every trick's prize is shown before the hand starts. All numbers are placeholders.

- Four suits (● ■ ▲ ◆), ranks 1 to 6. Each player has its own deck of 24 cards: one of every suit and rank.
- A face-up card costs energy by rank: 1–2 cost 0, 3–4 cost 1, 5 costs 2, 6 costs 3.
- **A hand.** Each player shuffles its own deck and deals itself 8 cards, with 10 energy for the hand (unspent energy is lost when the hand ends). The trump suit is ● in hand 1, ■ in hand 2, then ▲, ◆, ● and so on. The prizes 1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3 are shuffled and laid out in order: trick 1 is worth the first, trick 2 the second, and so on. The first leader alternates from hand to hand; after that each trick's winner leads.
- **A trick.** The leader plays a card face-up, paying its cost, or any card face-down for free. The follower: if it holds a card of the led suit it can pay for, it must play one of those; otherwise it plays any card it can pay for face-up, or any card face-down for free.
- A face-down card has no suit and no rank and never wins, except that two face-down cards give the trick to the leader. After a face-down lead, any face-up card from the follower wins. Otherwise the highest trump wins; with no trump, the highest card of the led suit. The winner scores the trick's prize.
- **End.** At the end of a hand, a player on 20 or more wins; both on 20 or more: the higher wins, equal plays another hand. After 5 hands the higher score wins; equal is a draw.
