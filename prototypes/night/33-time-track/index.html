Time track

No turns. Whoever's marker is further behind on the track acts next (on the same space, the one that arrived last). Every action moves your marker forward by its time.

Play a card: its time (the blue number). A unit can attack once your marker reaches its ready time (shown on it and on the track): 3 after it is played.
Attack: tap a ready unit, then an enemy unit or the enemy. Time 1. Both units deal their attack to each other; damage stays. The unit is ready again after its recovery (the small number).
Draw: tap your deck. Time 2. Hand max 8.

Hit 3, Hit 6: damage to a unit or the enemy. Haste: one of your units is ready now.

Life 0 loses. Markers stop at 60; when both are there, higher life wins.

If you can do nothing at all, you wait (time 1).
