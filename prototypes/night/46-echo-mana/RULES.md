# Echo mana

Bring the enemy hero from 30 health to 0.

- **Mana.** At the start of your turn your mana is what the opponent spent on their last turn, plus 1: at least 2, at most 10. The first player's first turn has 2. Unspent mana is lost. Whatever you spend, your opponent gets next turn, plus 1; the side not moving shows its coming mana as it grows.
- Each turn: draw 1, then play cards and attack in any order. Opening hands 3 (first player) and 4 (second), and the second player gets the Coin (0 mana: +1 mana this turn; what you pay with it counts as spent).
- Up to 7 units a side. A unit can attack from the turn after it is played, once a turn, any enemy unit or the enemy hero. Units fighting deal their attack to each other; damage stays.
- Empty deck: drawing deals 1 damage to you, then 2, then 3.
