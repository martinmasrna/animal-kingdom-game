Bust duel

Life 15 each; 15 rounds, then higher life wins.

Lines. Taking turns one card at a time, each player either draws (the top card of their own deck goes face-up into their line; an empty deck reshuffles the discard) or stops. Once you stop or bust, the other continues alone.

Bust. Red dots are strain. Strain 4 or more and your line counts as 0. Calm's green dot takes 1 away.

Clash. The higher line wins; the loser loses the difference in life (at least 1, at most 5). Equal: nothing.

Buy. Each player may buy one market card costing up to its line's power (2 if busted); it goes to the discard. The loser buys first.

Spoil: on draw, the opponent's line gains 1 strain, even if they stopped. Peek: on draw, see your top card. Trim: when bought, remove a card in your discard from the game.

Every deck starts with Step 1 ×4, Step 2 ×3, Risk 3 ×2, Crack ×1. The numbers by each deck list what is left in it.
