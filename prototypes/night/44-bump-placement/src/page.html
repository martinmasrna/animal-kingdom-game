Bump Placement

First to 20 points at the end of a round wins (both: the higher; equal plays on). After round 12 the higher score wins. Same 24-card deck for both; each starts with 5 cards.

Each round both draw 3 (more than 8 cards: discard down), get 4 mana (+3 if their card was on Fund last round). Unspent mana is lost. The starting player alternates every round.

Place or pass, taking turns from the starting player. Pay a card's cost and put it on a space. A space holds one card: a stronger card bumps a weaker enemy card back to its owner's hand; you can't place on your own card or on an enemy card as strong or stronger. Once you pass you place nothing more this round.

When both have passed, the spaces pay their owners left to right: Score 3, Score 1 (+1 and draw 1), Study (draw 2), Fund (+3 mana next round), Sabotage (the opponent discards a random card). Then every placed card goes to its owner's discard pile.

Lock can't be bumped. Shove, when placed, bumps an enemy card of strength 3 or less from another space.
