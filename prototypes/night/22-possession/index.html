Possession

You attack to the right. Score by Shooting from the last three zones before the goal. Most goals after 20 plays wins.

Every play both of you choose at once: whoever has the ball an attacking option, the other a defending one. Each costs stamina (●). Basics are always there; cards come from your hand and you draw a new one after playing it. You start with 10 stamina and get 4 back after play 10.
