Junk War

Both start with 7 Coin and 3 Score. Five market cards lie face-up; a bought card is replaced at once. First to 25 points wins at once.

Your turn: play every card in your hand, in any order: coin goes to this turn's pool, points are scored at once, effects happen. Then buy any number of market cards with the coin; they go to your discard pile. Then discard everything and draw 5 (shuffle your discard when the deck runs out).

Junk does nothing. Foul and Foul 2 send Junk from a shared pile of 30 into the other player's deck; Purge and Clean remove cards from the game. Draw: draw 2 cards and play them. Thief: +2 coin and take 1 point from the other player.

The first player's first hand is 3 cards. When the market deck runs out, the round is finished so both have had the same number of turns, and more points wins.
