Simultaneous Turns

  • Life 20. Bring the other player to 0; both at once is a draw.
  • Each round both players' mana is the round number (up to 10) and both draw 1. An empty deck costs 1 life, then 2, then 3.
  • Both plan the round at the same time, then press Go. Plan which cards to play (within your mana; up to 6 units), a target for each Hit, and for each of your units an enemy unit or the enemy player to attack, or nothing.
  • Tap a unit card to play it. Tap a Hit, then its target. Tap your unit, then what it attacks; tap it twice to keep it home.
  • Then everything resolves: first all Hits at once; then all new units enter (they attack from next round); then all attacks at once. An attacked unit deals its attack back to every unit attacking it. Damage stays. An attack whose attacker or target was destroyed by a Hit does not happen.
  • Deck, the same for both, 3 of each: Striker 1 mana 2/1, Raider 2: 3/2, Guard 2: 2/3, Line 3: 3/4, Heavy 4: 4/5, Big 5: 5/5, Huge 6: 6/7, Hit 2 (1 mana), Hit 3 (2), Hit 6 (4), each to any unit or player.
