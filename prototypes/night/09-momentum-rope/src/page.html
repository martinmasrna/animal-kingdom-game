Momentum rope

Push the marker to the right end to win; your opponent pushes it left. It moves at once whenever it reaches an end.

Each turn your mana goes up by 1 (max 8) and refills, and you draw 1 (an empty deck moves the marker 1 toward your own end). Play cards by paying their cost.

At the end of every turn, the marker moves by the velocity (the arrow, at most 4 either way).

Shove and Jolt push the marker now. Heave and Big Heave add velocity toward the opponent. Check stops it; Reverse flips it; Surge moves the marker by the velocity now. Anchor: until your next turn, nothing can move the marker toward your end.

After 12 rounds the side the marker is on wins (on 0, the player the arrow points toward loses).

The second player starts with 4 cards and the Coin (+1 mana once).
