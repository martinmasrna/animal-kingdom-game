Score or Fight

First to 40 points wins. There is no hero and no life.

Each turn: mana +1 (up to 10) and refills, your units get ready, draw 1. Play cards by paying their cost; up to 6 units. A unit cannot act the turn it is played.

Each ready unit does one thing, then is spent until your next turn:

  • Attack an enemy unit: both deal their attack to each other. Damage stays.
  • Score: add its current health to your points (tap the unit, then your points).

Hit cards damage an enemy unit. The second player starts with 4 cards and the Coin (+1 mana once); the first with 3. An empty deck gives the opponent 2 points, then 4, then 6.
