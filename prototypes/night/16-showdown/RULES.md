# Showdown

Chips are your life: 20 each. Take all of the opponent's chips, or have more after 20 rounds.

## A round

- **Ante.** Each player puts 1 chip in the pot (2 from round 6, 3 from round 11; the round track shows it). A player who cannot pay loses.
- **Draw** up to 5 cards (the discard is shuffled back in when the deck runs out). Cards you do not buy stay in your hand.
- **Buy.** At the same time, each player picks up to 3 cards from hand as its line and pays their costs from 6 mana. Then the mana each spent is shown; the lines stay face-down.
- **Bet.** The round's first player (it alternates) checks or bets 2. After a check, the other checks (showdown) or bets 2. Facing a bet: call, raise (2 more than the bet; once per player per round), or fold.
- **Showdown.** Both lines turn over; the higher total power takes the pot. A tie splits it, an odd chip to the second player. A fold gives the pot to the other player and the folded line is never shown.
- Bought cards go to the discard pile.
