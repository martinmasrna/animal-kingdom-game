# 03 Exert to score: rules

Two players, the same 30-card deck each. First to 20 points wins, the moment it happens.

**Setup.** Shuffle. Random first player; the first player draws 3, the second draws 4 and gets the Coin (0 mana: gain 1 mana this turn).

**Your turn.**

1. Ready all your units. Your maximum mana goes up by 1 (cap 10) and refills. Draw 1. Drawing from an empty deck gives the opponent 2 points, then 4, then 6.
2. In any order, as often as you can pay or act:
   - Play a card by paying its cost. A unit enters ready (max 6 units) and cannot act this turn.
   - Each ready unit that was not played this turn may take one action, which exerts it:
     - **Score**: gain its Score in points.
     - **Challenge**: choose an exerted enemy unit; both deal their Strength to each other. Damage stays; a unit at 0 Health is destroyed.
3. End the turn.

Ready units cannot be challenged. Exerted units stay exerted until their owner's next turn begins, so a unit that scored or challenged can be challenged during the opponent's turn. Hits can target any unit, ready or exerted.

**Cards (30, both players).** Cost · Strength/Health · Score.

| Card | Cost | Str/Health | Score | Copies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Runner | 1 | 1/1 | 1 | 3 |
| Scout | 2 | 1/3 | 2 | 3 |
| Raider | 2 | 3/2 | 0 | 3 |
| Guard | 3 | 2/5 | 1 | 3 |
| Line | 3 | 3/3 | 2 | 3 |
| Heavy | 4 | 4/5 | 2 | 3 |
| Big | 5 | 5/6 | 2 | 2 |
| Star | 6 | 3/6 | 4 | 2 |
| Hit 2 | 1 | 2 damage to any unit | | 3 |
| Hit 4 | 3 | 4 damage to any unit | | 2 |
| Draw 2 | 2 | Draw 2 cards | | 3 |

A turn cap of 120 turns ends the game as a draw (never reached in the gate).
