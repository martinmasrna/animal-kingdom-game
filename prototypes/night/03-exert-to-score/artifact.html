Exert to score

First to 20 points wins.

Each turn: your units ready, your mana goes up by 1 (max 10) and refills, you draw 1. Empty deck: the opponent gains 2, then 4, then 6.

Play cards by paying their cost. A unit can't act the turn it is played. Each other ready unit may act once, which exerts it (turned sideways):

Score: tap the unit, then your points; gain its Score (the gold number).
Challenge: tap the unit, then an exerted enemy unit; both deal their Strength (left) to each other's Health (right). Damage stays.

Only exerted units can be challenged. Units stay exerted until their owner's next turn. Hits hit any unit. Max 6 units.

The second player starts with 4 cards and the Coin (+1 mana once).
