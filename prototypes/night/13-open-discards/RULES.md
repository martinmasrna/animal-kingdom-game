# Open discards

Bring the enemy hero from 30 health to 0.

- **Open discards.** Each player has a face-up discard pile beside their hero. A destroyed unit and a resolved spell go to the discard pile of the player who played them. Once per turn you may play one card from the opponent's discard pile, paying its cost plus 1 (its gem there shows that price). It works as if it were yours, and when it later dies or resolves it goes to your pile. The Coin leaves the game when used.
- Mana: 1 on your first turn, one more each turn up to 10, refilled each turn.
- Each turn: draw 1, then play cards and attack in any order. Opening hands 3 (first player) and 4 (second), and the second player gets the Coin (0 mana: +1 mana this turn).
- Up to 7 units a side. A unit can attack from the turn after it is played, once a turn, any enemy unit or the enemy hero. Units fighting deal their attack to each other; damage stays.
- Empty deck: drawing deals 1 damage to you, then 2, then 3.
