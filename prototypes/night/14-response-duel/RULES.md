# Response duel

No board. Spells hit the opponent; mana you keep pays for answers on the opponent's turn. All numbers are placeholders.

- Both players have the same 30-card deck, shuffled. Life 20, never above 20. Hand limit 10 (a card drawn beyond it is discarded).
- Random first player. The first player draws 3, the second draws 4 and gets the Coin (0 mana: gain 1 mana this turn).
- Your turn: Sieges you cast tick; your maximum mana goes up by 1 (to 10) and refills; you draw 1. Drawing from an empty deck loses 1 life, then 2, then 3, and so on. Cast Spells by paying their cost, then end the turn.
- **Stored mana.** Mana you did not spend stays available through the opponent's next turn, for Responses only, and is gone when your mana refills.
- **Responses.** Whenever a player casts a Spell, the other may cast one Response to it, paying from stored mana. The Response resolves first, then the Spell (unless cancelled). Nobody responds to a Response, and Responses are cast only this way.
- A Siege leaves an effect that ticks at the start of its caster's next three turns; it can be answered only when it is cast.
- Life 0 or less loses.

| Card | Type | Cost | Text | Copies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bolt | Spell | 1 | 2 damage to the opponent | 5 |
| Blast | Spell | 3 | 5 damage to the opponent | 3 |
| Barrage | Spell | 5 | 8 damage to the opponent | 2 |
| Drain | Spell | 2 | 2 damage to the opponent; you gain 2 life | 3 |
| Study | Spell | 2 | Draw 2 cards | 3 |
| Siege | Spell | 4 | At the start of each of your next 3 turns, 2 damage to the opponent | 2 |
| Cancel | Response | 3 | Cancel the spell | 4 |
| Ward | Response | 1 | The spell deals 3 less damage (a Siege 1 less each tick) | 5 |
| Mirror | Response | 2 | The spell's damage hits its caster instead (a damage spell of 5 or less; not Siege) | 3 |

The game is called a draw after 120 turns.
