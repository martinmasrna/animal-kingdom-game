Curling Line

A sheet of cells 1 to 12. The Mark is cell 9; the House is cells 6 to 12.

A throw slides from the left toward its target cell. If a stone stands on the way, the first one stops it on the cell before and is pushed on by what is left of the throw; a pushed stone that meets another stops before it and passes the rest on. Pushed past 12, or stopped on 0, is out.

An end: each player discards its hand, draws 5 and has 5 energy. Eight throws, alternating, the player without the hammer first. A throw plays a card (paying its energy) or the free Lag (to cell 4). A card throws to the cell on it; Any lets you pick a cell from 4 to 12.

Scoring: only House stones count. The player with the stone closest to the Mark scores 1 for each of its House stones closer than the other player's closest. Equal closest, or an empty House: no score. The player who did not score takes the hammer (the last throw); after no score, the hammer stays.

After 6 ends, more points wins; equal plays one more end.
