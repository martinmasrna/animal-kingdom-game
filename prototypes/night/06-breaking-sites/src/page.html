Breaking Sites

First to 15 points wins.

Three sites lie in the middle. Each shows the total power on it, its threshold, and two prizes: gold for the winner, silver for the runner-up. His units stand above a site, yours below (up to 4 each).

The moment the total power on a site, both sides together, reaches its threshold, it breaks: the side with more power there takes the gold points; the other side takes the silver points if it has a unit there; a tie gives both the silver. Its units are discarded and the next site takes its place.

Each turn: mana +1 (up to 10) and refills, draw 1. Play cards by paying their cost: a unit onto your side of any site; Move one of your units to another site; Strike destroys an enemy unit of power 3 or less; Boost gives one of your units +3 power for good; Draw 2.

The first player starts with 3 cards; the second with 4 and the Coin (+1 mana once). Drawing from an empty deck gives the opponent 1 point. When the site deck is empty and every site has broken, the higher score wins.
