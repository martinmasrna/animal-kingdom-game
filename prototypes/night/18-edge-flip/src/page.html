Edge Flip

A 4×4 grid. The player owning more cells when it is full wins (or when both pass in a row).

Both play the same 20-card deck. The first player starts with 4 cards, the second with 5 and the Coin (+1 mana this turn).

Your turn: mana +1 (up to 6) and refill, draw 1. Place any number of cards on empty cells, paying each one's cost. Tap a card to pick it, tap it again to turn it, then tap a cell.

When a card is placed, each of its edges is compared with the touching edge of every adjacent enemy card (up, down, left, right): where yours is higher, that card becomes yours. Taken cards take nothing further.
