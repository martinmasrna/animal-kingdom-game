Swing Neutrals

  • Life 15. Bring the other player to 0.
  • Three shared Neutrals stand on loyalty tracks. A Neutral below the middle leans your way, above it leans his. The number is its attack.
  • At the start of your turn, every Neutral leaning your way deals its attack to the other player.
  • Then your mana goes up by 1 (up to 8) and refills, and you draw 1. An empty deck costs 1 life, then 2, then 3.
  • Play cards by paying their cost. "Toward you" moves a Neutral one step your way; a track ends 3 steps from the middle.
  • The first player starts with 3 cards; the second with 4 and the Coin (gain 1 mana this turn).
