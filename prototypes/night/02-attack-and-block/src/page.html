Attack and Block

Life 20 each; bring the other to 0. Same 30-card deck for both. The first player starts with 3 cards, the second with 4 and the Coin (+1 mana this turn).

Your turn. Ready your units, mana +1 (up to 10) and refill, draw 1 (empty deck: lose 1 life, then 2, then 3). Play cards for their cost. A unit can't attack the turn it is played, but it can block on the next enemy turn.

Attack, once a turn. Tap your ready units, then Attack. They stay exhausted until your next turn, so they can't block.

Block. The defender gives each attacker at most one of its ready units: tap your unit, then the attacker; tap again to undo. Blocked units hit each other; unblocked attackers hit life. Damage at least a unit's health destroys it.

At end of turn every surviving unit heals fully. Max 6 units a side.

Hit 2 / Hit 4: damage to an enemy unit. Draw 2: draw two cards.
