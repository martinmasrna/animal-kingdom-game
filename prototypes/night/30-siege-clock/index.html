Siege clock

Two roles with their own decks; you take each in turn, game by game. The Attacker wins the moment the Gate falls from 20 to 0. The Defender wins if it still stands after round 10.

  • Each side starts with 4 cards. Mana each turn equals the round number, refilled. Draw 1 at the start of your turn (an empty deck draws nothing).
  • The Attacker's units stand in the Field (up to 5), the Defender's on the Wall (up to 4). Damage stays.

A round

  • Attacker's turn. Play cards, then send any Field units that were already there at the start of the turn (tap them; a Rush lets a unit played this turn go too). The Defender then blocks: each Wall unit may block one sent unit, one blocker per unit. A blocked unit and its blocker deal their attack to each other; an unblocked unit hits the Gate (a Ram for double).
  • A Wall unit that blocks is tired: it cannot block in the next round (shown dimmed). This is the one rule changed from the first design, where Wall units blocked every round.
  • Defender's turn. Play cards. At the end of the turn each Shooter deals 1 damage to a Field unit.
