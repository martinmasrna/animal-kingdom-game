Blind fronts

Commit cards face-down to three fronts, then reveal; the stronger side at each front takes its value.

  • Both players have the same 25-card deck, shuffled, and draw 5. Each starts with 4 energy; energy banks between rounds, up to 10.
  • Round 1's first player is random; the first player alternates every round.
  • A round. Both gain 4 energy and draw 2 (hand limit 8: discard down by choice; a discard pile is reshuffled into the deck when the deck runs out). The fronts get the values 1, 2 and 3 in a random order, shown.
  • Placement. Starting with the round's first player, players alternate: place one card face-down at a front, paying its cost, or pass. A player who has passed places nothing more this round. Both see how many cards sit at each front and how much energy each has left (so what each placed card cost).
  • When both have passed, everything is revealed. At each front the higher total strength wins the front's value in points; equal: nobody. All placed cards go to their owners' discard piles.
  • End. After a round in which a player reaches 12 points, the higher score wins; equal scores play another round. After round 12 the higher score wins; equal is a draw.
