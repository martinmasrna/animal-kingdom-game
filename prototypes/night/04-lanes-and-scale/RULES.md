# Lanes and scale

Four lanes. Each player has one slot per lane on their side, so each lane has two facing slots. A shared scale starts level; five toward you wins at once. All numbers are placeholders.

- Both players have the same 30-card deck, shuffled. Random first player. The first player draws 3, the second draws 4 and gets the Coin (0 mana: gain 1 mana this turn).
- Your turn: your maximum mana goes up by 1 (to 10) and refills, and you draw 1. Drawing from an empty deck moves the scale 1 toward your opponent.
- Play cards by paying their cost. A unit goes into an empty slot on your side. Spells resolve at once.
- **Combat** ends your turn: each of your units, lane 1 to lane 4, attacks. Facing an enemy unit, it deals its attack to that unit, which does not strike back. Facing an empty slot, it moves the scale by its attack toward you. Damage stays; a unit at 0 health is removed at once. Units attack on the turn they are played. Only your units attack in your combat.
