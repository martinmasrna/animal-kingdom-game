Range Duel

Two fighters on a line of 7 spaces, 12 life each. Bring the other to 0; after 20 turns the higher life wins.

Each turn both gain 2 energy (max 6), then both pick one move in secret: a card from hand or a basic. Moves are shown together and resolve by speed, lowest first; equal speeds at once (both move, then both hit).

A move first moves you (→ toward the other fighter, ← away; you can't enter or pass their space), then hits if the distance is inside its range (adjacent = 1). A hit on a fighter whose move has not resolved yet cancels that move.

Cards: top left energy cost, top right speed, big number damage, bottom range and movement. Guard: damage to you this turn is 3 less. Sweep pushes the other 1 away on a hit.

Basics: Step (speed 3) one space either way. Rest (speed 5): +1 energy next turn. After each turn both draw 1.
