Front and Back

Life 20 each; bring the other to 0. Same 30-card deck for both. The first player starts with 3 cards, the second with 4 and the Coin (+1 mana this turn).

Your turn. Mana +1 (up to 10) and refill, units ready, draw 1 (empty deck: lose 1 life, then 2, then 3). Play cards for their cost. A unit can't attack the turn it is played.

Rows. Melee units go to your front row, Ranged units to your back row, 3 each at most.

Attack. Each ready unit attacks once. It must hit an enemy front-row unit; only when the enemy front row is empty can it hit the back row or the player. A Melee attacker and its target deal their attack to each other; a Ranged attacker takes nothing back. Damage stays; 0 health destroys a unit.

Hit 2 / Hit 4: damage to any enemy unit, whatever its row.
