Supply Grid

Your Base (power 3) is at the bottom, the enemy's at the top. Destroy the enemy Base, or have more pieces on the grid (Bases included) after round 12.

Each turn: mana +1 (up to 8) and refills, draw 1. Then in any order:

  • Place a unit by paying its cost, on an empty square next to one of your pieces.
  • Strike: each of your pieces, the Base and new units included, may strike once per turn at a neighbouring enemy piece. The lower power is destroyed; equal powers destroy both. A Wall cannot strike.
  • Cut: destroy an enemy unit of power 2 or less.

A piece no longer connected to its Base through its own pieces (side by side, not diagonal) is removed at once.

The first player starts with 3 cards; the second with 4 and the Coin (+1 mana once).
