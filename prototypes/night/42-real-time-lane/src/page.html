Real-Time Lane

One lane, your base against the other's, 20 health each. The clock runs: 3 minutes. Destroy the other base, or have more base health when time runs out (level: one more minute, and the first base damage decides).

Mana starts at 5 and grows by 1 every 2 seconds, up to 10. Cards: both decks hold the same 8 cards; you hold 4, the small card is next. A card you play goes to the back and the next one comes in.

Tap a card to play it. Units appear at your base and walk toward the other base. A unit hits the nearest enemy unit, Wall or base within its range ahead, otherwise it walks on, never past an enemy. Wall: tap a spot in your half; enemy units stop at it. Blast: tap a spot; 8 damage to every enemy unit within 1.5.

Numbers per half second: Runner 4 health, 1 damage, fast. Grunt 10/2. Shooter 4/1, range 3. Brute 14/3, slow. Tank 30/1, slowest. Swarm three of 2/1. Wall 20.
