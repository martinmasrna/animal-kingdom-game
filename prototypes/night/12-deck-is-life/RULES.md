# Deck is life

There is no life total: your deck is your life. All numbers are placeholders.

- Both players have the same 30-card deck, shuffled. Deck sizes are always shown. Up to 6 units a side.
- Random first player. The first player draws 3, the second draws 4 and gets the Coin (0 mana: gain 1 mana this turn; it then leaves the game).
- Your turn: your maximum mana goes up by 1 (to 10) and refills, your units become ready, you draw 1. Then play cards by paying their cost and attack with each ready unit once. A unit cannot attack on the turn it is played.
- An attack hits an enemy unit (both deal their attack to each other; damage stays) or the enemy player.
- Damage to a player banishes that many cards from the top of their deck, face-up, out of the game for good.
- Destroyed units and used spells go to their owner's discard pile, face-up, never reshuffled.
- A player who must draw from an empty deck (the turn's draw or a Draw 2) loses at once.
