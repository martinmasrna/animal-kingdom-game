Price Tracks

  • Three goods, X, Y and Z, each with a shared price from 1 to 9, starting at 4. Your deck: 5 of each good, 2 Boost, 2 Crash, 1 Spy.
  • Your turn: gain 2 coin (you hold at most 6), draw up to 5 cards (your discard pile is shuffled in when needed).
  • Sell once a turn: any number of goods of one type from your hand. You score their price times the number sold, then that price drops by the number sold (not below 1). Tap the goods, then their track.
  • Boost (1 coin): a price +2. Crash (2 coin): a price −3. Spy (1 coin): see the other player's hand.
  • After both players' turns, every price rises by 1 (up to 9).
  • When a player reaches 40 points, the match ends at the end of that round and the higher score wins. Otherwise it ends after 14 rounds.
