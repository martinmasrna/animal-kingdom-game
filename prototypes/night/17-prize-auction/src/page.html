Prize Auction

16 prizes, one a round. Most points at the end wins (tie: more coin owned).

You both start with the same 8 coin cards (six 1s, two 2s); 5 in hand. Each round both draw 2 (an empty deck reshuffles your discard). Over 7 cards: discard down to 7.

Bid: pick any cards from your hand, in secret; both bids are shown at once. The higher bid takes the prize, pays its bid cards to its discard, and the prize goes to its discard too: from then on you draw it. The lower bidder keeps its cards. Equal bids: nobody takes it and it stays, joined by the next prize.

Debt (−3) goes the other way: with Debt up, the lower bidder takes everything up, and every bid comes back.

Coin prizes make your money stronger; point prizes score but sit in your deck as 0. Messenger: when you draw it, draw 1 more. The next prize is shown beside the one up now.
