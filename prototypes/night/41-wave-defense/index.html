Wave Defense

Threats walk down three lanes, rows 4 to 1, toward your Wall (20). Each lane has one Defender slot at the Wall.

Each round: threats advance 1 row (Swift 2); only one threat can stand in row 1 of a lane, the rest wait in row 2. Each threat in row 1 attacks: the lane's Defender and the threat deal their attack to each other; with no Defender, it hits the Wall. Then new threats arrive in row 4 of the emptiest lane (1 a round, 2 from round 7).

Your turn: 4 mana every round. Draw 2 (hand max 7). Play cards: Defenders go into a slot (replacing the one there), spells resolve at once. When you end the turn, each Defender with Shoot hits the nearest threat in its lane.

Hit 3, Hit 6: damage to a threat. Bomb: 2 damage to every threat in one lane. Freeze: threats in one lane do not advance next round. Repair, Rebuild: Wall +4, +8 (max 20).

After rounds 4, 8 and 12, take one of three cards into your deck, or none.

Win when the threat deck is empty and no threat is left. Lose when the Wall reaches 0. The Expert plays the same game alongside you; the two numbers count who ended better (a win beats a loss, then more Wall, then more rounds).
