Pitch to Pay

  • Life 30. Bring the other player to 0.
  • There is no mana. To pay for a card, pitch other cards from your hand: each adds its gold number to your pool for this turn and goes under your deck. Tap a card, then the pool.
  • The blue number is the cost. Mana left at the end of the turn is lost.
  • A unit cannot attack the turn it is played. Each ready unit attacks once: an enemy unit (both deal their attack to each other, damage stays) or the enemy player.
  • End of turn: draw up to 5. An empty deck reshuffles the discard pile.
  • From your 15th turn on you lose 1 life at its start, then 2, then 3…
  • The first player starts with 4 cards, the second with 5. Up to 6 units a side.
