Round Purse

Best of three rounds. Both decks are the same 20 cards; each player draws 8, and 2 more at the start of rounds 2 and 3 (hand max 10).

One purse of 18 mana pays for every card of the whole match. It never refills.

Players alternate turns: play one card (paying its cost) or pass. A player who has passed is out of the round; the other keeps playing until it passes too. Then the higher total power wins the round; equal, nobody wins it. The table is cleared.

Gift (free): 4 power on the other side this round, and you draw 2. Cut: destroy the other side's strongest card this round.

First to win 2 rounds wins. The starter alternates: round 1's starter starts round 3.
