Commander Grid

Each side has a Commander (2 attack, 15 health). Bring the enemy Commander to 0. Same 24-card deck for both. The first player starts with 3 cards, the second with 4 and the Coin (+1 mana this turn).

Your turn. Mana +1 (up to 8) and refill, draw 1. From round 15 your Commander first takes 1 damage, 2 in round 16, and so on.

Play cards for their cost. A unit goes onto an empty cell next to your Commander (not diagonal) and can't act this turn.

Act. Each of your pieces, the Commander too, may move 1 cell (not diagonal) to an empty cell and then attack an enemy piece next to it: both deal their attack to each other. Attacking ends its turn. Damage stays.

Line: moves up to 2. Lancer: attacks a piece exactly 2 cells away in a straight line, over anything between, and takes nothing back; it can't attack next to it. Hit 3: 3 damage to an enemy piece within 2 cells of your Commander. Rally: a piece that has moved or attacked may do so again.
