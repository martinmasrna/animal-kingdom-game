Hidden Contracts

No life: first to 10 points wins. Points come only from contracts. Each player holds 2 face-down contracts drawn from their own deck of 8; the opponent cannot see yours.

Your turn. Mana +1 (up to 10) and refill, units ready, draw 1 (empty deck: the opponent gets 1 point). Then every contract you hold whose condition is true is revealed and scores; you draw a replacement.

Play cards for their cost (max 6 units). A unit can't attack the turn it is played. Once a turn you may pay 1 mana to bury a held contract face-down and draw a new one.

Attack. Each ready unit may attack one enemy unit: both deal their attack to each other. Damage stays.

When both players have no contracts left to hold or draw, the higher score wins.

Hit 2 / 3 / 6: damage to an enemy unit.
